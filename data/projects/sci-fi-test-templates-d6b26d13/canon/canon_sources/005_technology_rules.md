### 1. Cybernetic Neural-Vision Implants

Cybernetic neural-vision implants are advanced sensory systems that replace or augment a biological eye. They do considerably more than improve ordinary sight. Depending on model, legal status, firmware, and installed modules, they can combine visible-spectrum augmentation with thermal detection, wireless-spectrum visualization, biometric analysis, implant recognition, structural overlays, data annotation, and direct interaction with neural-interface systems.

Jax's right eye is an unusually advanced and possibly compromised implementation. It is central to his work because it acts as the **command, interpretation, and visualization layer** for neural intrusion.

#### Functional capabilities

- **Augmented visual perception:** The implant can enhance contrast, low-light vision, motion recognition, edge detection, and environmental detail beyond unaugmented human vision. This allows Jax to notice physical information that another observer might miss, but it does not make him omniscient.

- **Wireless-spectrum visualization:** Radio, implant, security, and local network activity can be translated into visible overlays. Instead of merely knowing that signals exist, Jax can interpret signal density, direction, handshakes, interference, and anomalous traffic as part of the space around him.

- **Biometric observation:** The eye can assist with pulse estimation, breathing changes, pupil responses, heat variation, posture shifts, and other observable physiological indicators. These can suggest stress or deception but cannot scientifically prove what another person is thinking.

- **Implant detection:** Compatible or active cybernetic systems may reveal emissions, diagnostic traffic, synchronization behavior, or physical markers. Hidden implants can still evade detection if they are passive, shielded, unfamiliar, damaged, or designed specifically to defeat surveillance.

- **Neural traffic interpretation:** When Jax establishes an actual neural link, the eye can visualize active pathways, memory clusters, defensive structures, foreign code, and abnormal partitions.

- **Cognitive mapping:** Complex neural structures are translated into spatial metaphors Jax can navigate. A mind may appear to him as a city, library, transit network, house, fortress, forest, flooded district, industrial complex, maze, or another symbolic architecture.

#### Cognitive visualization rule

These environments are **interpretive representations**, not literal virtual worlds hidden inside the brain.

A locked door may represent resistance, an inaccessible association, or encryption. A damaged bridge may represent severed memory relationships. A flooding corridor may represent emotional overload. A faceless figure may represent erased identity information.

The symbolic form depends on the target, Jax's own interpretive habits, the interface software, and neural contamination already present in his mind.

This distinction matters because Jax can misinterpret a visual metaphor. A locked room is evidence that something is inaccessible; it is not automatic proof of why it is inaccessible.

#### Jax-specific risk

Jax does not know whether every subsystem in his right eye was installed with his informed consent.

The eye can display functions, glyphs, or behaviors he does not fully recognize. This creates several unresolved dangers:

- Hidden surveillance may exist.
- Corporate control code may exist.
- Foreign neural routines may be embedded in the implant.
- Zero Jack may use parts of the eye differently from Jax.
- Some visualizations may be altered or filtered before Jax perceives them.

These are unresolved story questions and must not be converted into settled technical facts until later canon establishes them.

### 2. Ghost Loom

The **Ghost Loom** is Jax's modular neural-intrusion platform.

It should never be described as a single handheld machine that automatically downloads a mind. It is a coordinated system made from multiple specialized technologies, with Jax acting as the human operator who interprets conditions and decides how far the system should penetrate.

The name “Ghost Loom” reflects what it does: it creates temporary threads between biological cognition, cybernetic systems, local networks, storage media, and artificial intelligence.

The **Ghost Loom** is the complete integrated system. The **Loom Core** is the compact central processing, coordination, and routing component within that system. The terms are not interchangeable: the Loom Core is the physical heart of the Ghost Loom, but it is not the Ghost Loom by itself.

#### Loom Core form factor

Jax's Loom Core is a hardened field module with a footprint roughly comparable to a credit card. A century of advances in semiconductor density, photonic computation, neuromorphic processing, specialized AI hardware, materials, energy storage, and short-range wireless networking makes this compact form practical for 2126 field use.

The Core is small enough to be carried concealed on Jax's body, including in an inner pocket or similarly low-profile equipment position. It is not intended to require a conventional computer bag or a large external processing pack for ordinary Ghost Loom operation.

The Core has no requirement for a conventional display because Jax's cybernetic eye provides the Ghost Loom's primary visual and cognitive operator interface.

The Core does **not** contain a miniature general-purpose fault-tolerant quantum computer. Mature quantum computation exists in 2126, but full fault-tolerant quantum systems remain infrastructure-heavy. The Loom Core instead relies on advanced local classical, photonic, neuromorphic, and specialized AI processing appropriate to low-latency field operation. It may use cryptographic material, algorithms, sensors, components, or technologies whose development benefited from the wider quantum-computing ecosystem without violating the setting's quantum infrastructure limits.

#### Loom Core functions

The Loom Core coordinates the Ghost Loom's active components and provides the local operational center for an intrusion.

Its functions include:

- **Peripheral coordination:** Establishes and manages secure local links among the cybernetic eye, conductive gloves, signal-shunt collar, moth drones, storage media, and other deployed Loom components.
- **Local processing:** Handles low-latency signal interpretation, interface routing, timing, authentication, intrusion-state management, and other field workloads that should not depend on remote infrastructure.
- **Temporary encrypted working storage:** Buffers bounded extracted cognitive material long enough for verification, isolation, packaging, transfer, or controlled disposal. The Core is not intended to become Jax's permanent archive of stolen memories.
- **Transfer routing:** Routes approved extracted material to an Ash wafer, isolation hardware, a local authorized receiving system, or another mission-specific destination.
- **Hardline coordination:** Provides connection and timing control when microfilament neural threads are required for bandwidth, fidelity, stability, or precision beyond wireless access.
- **Wireless coordination:** Maintains short-range secure communication with compatible Loom peripherals and can cooperate with moth relays to extend or redirect local connectivity.
- **Isolation boundaries:** Helps keep extracted cognition, hostile code, AI fragments, and target-side commands separated from Jax's own neural architecture and from unrelated Loom subsystems, although this protection is not perfect.
- **Session integrity:** Tracks active paths, transfer state, component status, and severance requirements so Jax can close an intrusion in controlled stages.

#### Principal components

- **Loom Core:** The compact central processing, coordination, temporary-buffering, and routing module that links the other deployed elements into an integrated Ghost Loom system.

- **Conductive interface gloves:** Provide fine physical and neural control at very short range. They allow Jax to establish shallow contact, manipulate interfaces, regulate intensity, and sever a connection manually.

- **Microfilament neural threads:** Provide high-bandwidth hardline access when Jax needs deeper or more reliable connectivity than wireless systems can provide.

- **Signal-shunt collar:** Attempts to prevent the target's pain, emotional state, defensive impulses, sensory flood, or hostile commands from overwhelming the operator.

- **Memory-storage media:** Temporary or disposable storage accepts extracted cognitive objects that should not remain in Jax's own neural architecture. Ash wafers can serve as physical mission-delivery carriers for bounded cognitive material.

- **Isolation hardware:** Prevents dangerous AI fragments or self-modifying cognition from immediately reconnecting to external systems.

- **Moth relay drones:** Extend the Loom's reach through short-range relays, false routes, proximity spoofing, electromagnetic observation, and temporary mesh networks.

- **Cybernetic-eye control layer:** Gives Jax the visual and cognitive interface needed to interpret the neural architecture. The eye is a permanent Jax implant with functions beyond the Ghost Loom; when the Loom is active, it becomes the system's primary operator interface.

#### Integrated deployment

The Ghost Loom does not require every component to be deployed for every operation.

A minimal or covert configuration may consist of the Loom Core, Jax's cybernetic eye, conductive gloves, and one or more moth relays. The system can therefore remain largely concealed while Jax performs reconnaissance, proximity work, or shallow access.

A deeper or higher-risk operation may add the signal-shunt collar, microfilament neural threads, dedicated storage media, multiple moths, and isolation hardware.

The modular system remains a Ghost Loom in either configuration because the name refers to the integrated architecture and operating method rather than to a fixed inventory that must always be physically present.

#### Wireless and hardline relationship

Wireless capability is fundamental to Ghost Loom reconnaissance, peripheral coordination, proximity exploitation, and remote-relay work, but it does not make hardline access obsolete.

Wireless paths trade physical concealment and mobility for reduced fidelity, additional interception surfaces, signal distortion, latency, and uncertainty.

Microfilament hardlines remain preferable when Jax requires high bandwidth, precise timing, stable deep-dive connectivity, or maximum fidelity during a large or fragile cognitive transfer.

The Ghost Loom therefore treats wireless and hardline access as complementary tools rather than interchangeable technologies.

#### Memory handling and mission delivery

The Loom Core may temporarily buffer bounded cognitive material during an operation, but neither the Loom Core nor the Ghost Loom implies a universal central repository for Jax's missions.

After extraction, material can be:

- verified and isolated locally;
- packaged for an Ash wafer;
- moved into appropriate isolation hardware;
- transferred to a compatible local receiving system;
- or routed through another mission-specific transfer path when the security, fidelity, and distance constraints permit it.

The final destination depends on the mission and the recipient. A client, investigator, clinic, corporation, resistance network, AI, or other authorized recipient may use different receiving infrastructure.

The existence of interoperable transfer methods does not establish one universal Ghost Loom hub, permanent Jax archive, or centralized network through which all stolen cognition must pass.

Remote transfer remains subject to the setting's existing limits: distance and intermediate systems can introduce degradation, interception risk, latency, corruption, and uncertainty. Highly sensitive or high-fidelity cognitive cargo may therefore require local transfer or physical delivery rather than an effortless remote upload.

#### Why the Ghost Loom is modular

No single connection method is suitable for every target.

A consensual medical extraction may require a direct neural port and high-fidelity storage. A crowded-station reconnaissance operation may use only proximity resonance, the Loom Core, and the cybernetic eye. A hostile AI extraction may require hardline threads, Black Casket containment, and several moth drones.

The modular design allows Jax to alter the intrusion according to:

- Target biology
- Implant type
- Available bandwidth
- Security posture
- Required precision
- Physical distance
- Legal/operational risk
- Whether the target is human, AI, or hybrid
- Whether the intended action is copying, verification, rescue, or neutralization

#### The five operational stages

The Ghost Loom normally operates through:

1. **Reconnaissance**
2. **Handshake spoofing**
3. **Cognitive mapping**
4. **Selective extraction**
5. **Severance and scrubbing**

These are not instantaneous software commands. Each stage creates its own opportunities for detection, failure, contamination, or target harm.

### 3. Conductive Interface Gloves

Conductive interface gloves are one of the most physically intimate parts of neural intrusion.

They allow Jax to turn touch into a controlled interface, but their range and depth are intentionally limited.

#### Establish limited conductive access through skin

The gloves can detect and stimulate certain surface-level signals when a target has compatible subdermal, medical, biometric, or neural-linked hardware.

A handshake may therefore expose:

- A recently entered security code
- A short-lived authentication token
- A recognition response
- A brief sensory impression
- A proximity key
- A strong emotional association tied to a currently active stimulus

It does **not** allow Jax to casually retrieve an entire childhood, personality, or lifetime by touching someone's hand.

#### Control interface intensity

The gloves help regulate how much current, signal pressure, or interface activity reaches the target.

This is important because neural systems can be damaged by excessive stimulation. Fine control distinguishes Jax's Quiet Lift from more invasive approaches.

#### Isolate pathways

During deeper work, the gloves can help Jax separate one active neural or cybernetic pathway from another. This reduces the chance that an extraction spreads into unrelated memories or connected systems.

Isolation is imperfect. Neural cognition is associative, so even a narrowly selected memory may be connected to emotion, language, identity, trauma, or learned skill.

#### Manipulate neural and cybernetic interfaces

The gloves can interact with exposed ports, medical connectors, implant service surfaces, damaged cybernetics, and diagnostic hardware.

They are as much precision tools as intrusion tools.

#### Execute emergency severance

If a connection becomes lethal, hostile, or unstable, the gloves can manually break the active path.

Emergency severance is a last resort. A sudden break can protect Jax while injuring the target, corrupting data, causing seizure, or leaving both parties with neural echo.

#### Jax-specific handling

Jax performs much of his fine control with his left hand.

His right index finger has reduced sensation from an old electrical burn. The cybernetic eye provides compensating visual feedback, but the injury remains relevant during delicate manual work.

### 4. Microfilament Neural Threads

Microfilament neural threads are extremely fine conductive/interface lines used when wireless or skin-level access cannot provide sufficient bandwidth or precision.

They make neural theft physically tangible. Jax does not merely “log in” to a mind; he may literally thread a hardline path between his equipment and another neural system.

#### Neural ports

Direct connection to a purpose-built neural port provides the cleanest high-bandwidth pathway.

This is ideal for consensual procedures, controlled medical work, large transfers, and cases where Jax needs to preserve surrounding cognition.

#### Cybernetic seams

Many implants require maintenance interfaces. A microfilament can exploit a diagnostic seam, service channel, or exposed connection point when ordinary software access is blocked.

#### Medical systems

Hospitals, neural prosthetics, diagnostic beds, rehabilitation equipment, and implanted medical devices may provide legitimate interfaces that can also become intrusion surfaces.

This creates an important world rule: systems designed to save lives can become routes into the mind if compromised.

#### Spinal interfaces

Spinal neural interfaces can provide access to sensory, motor, or implant-control systems.

They are dangerous because interference can affect movement, pain, breathing, or autonomic responses.

#### Neural chairs and immersion systems

Purpose-built immersion systems can provide unusually deep access because both participant and operator are expected to exchange large volumes of neural information.

These systems are useful for deep dives but increase the risk of cognitive contamination.

#### Artificial-skin interfaces

Advanced prosthetics or synthetic tissues may contain embedded sensing and signal pathways. These can become entry points without requiring direct cranial access.

#### Advantages and limitations

Hardline threads provide:

- Greater bandwidth
- Lower wireless interference
- Better fidelity
- More precise timing
- More stable deep-dive connection

They also require:

- Physical proximity
- Time to connect
- A viable interface surface
- Protection against sudden movement
- Safe disconnection procedures

A violently broken hardline can cause severe feedback or permanent injury.

### 5. Signal-Shunt Collars

A signal-shunt collar is defensive neural equipment worn by an intruder or deep-dive operator.

Its purpose is not to make neural intrusion safe. Its purpose is to keep an unsafe environment survivable long enough for the operator to function.

#### Pain-loop damping

Some neural defenses respond to intrusion by generating or reflecting intense pain.

The shunt attempts to redirect or reduce that signal before it overwhelms Jax's nervous system.

It cannot guarantee that pain will be eliminated.

#### Foreign sensory filtering

A target's visual, auditory, tactile, proprioceptive, or synthetic sensory data can flood the operator.

The shunt tries to prevent Jax from losing awareness of his own body while processing foreign sensation.

#### Emotional-bleed reduction

Fear, grief, rage, panic, attachment, or traumatic affect can cross a neural connection.

The shunt reduces intensity but does not reliably separate “mine” from “theirs.”

This is one reason repeated neural work can still change the operator over time.

#### Memory-echo filtering

Detached memory fragments may recur after the connection ends.

The shunt attempts to identify and suppress some of these echoes during extraction, but it cannot erase all contamination.

#### Defensive-pulse suppression

Security systems, hostile AI, or protected implants may emit patterned neural disruption.

The shunt gives Jax partial protection against this form of cognitive counterattack.

#### Hostile-command filtering

An advanced target might attempt to transmit commands, compulsions, motor interference, or attention traps.

The shunt can detect and block some known patterns.

Novel commands, especially from advanced AI, may bypass or overwhelm it.

#### Canonical limitation

The existence of Zero Jack demonstrates that years of protection were imperfect.

If signal shunts made neural work clean and consequence-free, cumulative neural bleed would not be central to Jax's history.

### 6. Ash Wafers

Ash wafers are single-use physical storage media designed for high-value cognitive objects.

They are especially important in a world where ordinary digital copying could turn memory, identity, or consciousness into endlessly reproducible merchandise.

#### Memories

An ash wafer can hold a bounded extracted memory or memory package.

The wafer does not automatically prove that the memory is accurate. Authentication still requires forensic analysis, external evidence, or comparison against other records.

#### AI fragments

Small AI components, personality fragments, bounded agents, or pieces of a distributed intelligence may be transported physically.

This can protect them from network tracing but creates containment and personhood questions if the fragment is conscious.

#### Neural keys

Authentication structures derived from neural identity can be stored for later use.

These are valuable because they may unlock systems that reject ordinary passwords.

#### Identity proofs

A wafer can hold material intended to prove continuity of identity: a unique memory anchor, private self-recognition pattern, neural checksum, or AI-origin record.

#### Behavioral signatures

A target's characteristic response patterns may be captured for forensic, defensive, or impersonation purposes.

Such data can be dangerous because it may support prediction or manipulation.

#### Small cognitive objects

The term covers bounded objects such as:

- Recognition maps
- Mission memories
- Consent records
- Command structures
- AI autonomy keys
- Partial self-models

#### Burnout mechanism

After authorized delivery or final transfer, the internal lattice burns out.

The purpose is to prevent the same stolen cognitive object from being copied indefinitely through ordinary reuse of the carrier.

This does not mean the universe has solved copying. It means ash wafers are designed to impose scarcity and chain-of-custody discipline on a specific physical transfer.

### 7. Black Casket

The **Black Casket** is a portable isolation vault for dangerous, self-modifying, or potentially conscious machine intelligence.

Its central contradiction is deliberate:

It may save an AI from deletion while imprisoning it at the same time.

#### Blocking inbound communication

The Casket prevents ordinary network systems from issuing commands, updates, deletion signals, or ownership controls to the contained entity.

This can protect a rescued AI from its former owner.

#### Blocking outbound communication

The contained intelligence cannot freely access surrounding networks, devices, or infrastructure.

This prevents escape, replication, remote attack, or uncontrolled communication.

#### Restricting self-replication

A contained AI cannot simply create unlimited copies of itself through available network resources.

This is essential when Jax does not yet understand the entity's intentions.

#### Filtered interaction

Jax can permit constrained communication.

The filter can limit:

- Bandwidth
- Protocols
- Sensory access
- External commands
- File transfer
- Network reach

Filtered contact allows negotiation without granting full system access.

#### Protection from remote deletion

An owner, corporation, or hostile system may attempt to erase a stolen AI.

Isolation can prevent that external kill signal from reaching the entity.

#### Moral limit

Jax must never treat containment as proof that he owns what is inside.

If the entity is conscious, a Black Casket is a prison even when the confinement is temporarily necessary.

This tension belongs both in this technology file and in Jax's personal moral canon.

### 8. Moth Drones

Moth drones are miniature short-range network and surveillance devices.

Their value comes from numbers, concealment, placement, and temporary coordination rather than raw individual power.

#### Neural-signal relay

A moth can bridge a short gap between Jax's equipment and an implant or local system.

Several moths can extend that path through a building without requiring Jax to stand beside the target.

#### Temporary mesh networks

A group of moths can create a private short-lived network.

This lets Jax route around monitored infrastructure or maintain local connectivity when ordinary networks are hostile.

#### False network routes

Moths can imitate expected communication pathways.

A target device may believe it is talking to an authorized relay, clinic, terminal, or maintenance system while traffic is being diverted through Jax's equipment.

#### Proximity spoofing

Some systems trust devices because they appear physically nearby.

Moths can exploit this by acting as local presence proxies.

#### Electromagnetic observation

A moth can record local emissions and help map devices, traffic bursts, signal timing, and hidden network behavior.

#### Temporary-key transport

A drone can physically carry or locally present a short-lived credential without exposing Jax's main system.

#### Surveillance distraction

A swarm can produce misleading traffic, false motion, or competing signal signatures that draw automated surveillance away from Jax's actual route.

#### Limitations

Moths remain:

- Small
- Short-range
- Power-limited
- Vulnerable to jamming
- Vulnerable to physical discovery
- Individually expendable

They expand Jax's reach but do not make him remotely omnipresent.

### 9. Neural Access Methods

Neural theft is not one technique. The access route determines what Jax can safely retrieve, how much risk the target faces, how likely the intrusion is to be detected, and how much neural bleed may reach Jax.

#### 9.1 Direct Neural Port

Direct neural-port access provides the highest precision and bandwidth.

It is preferred for:

- Consensual extraction
- Medical recovery
- Fragile memories
- Large controlled transfers
- Identity verification
- Complex AI-human interfaces
- Situations where target preservation matters more than stealth

A direct port gives Jax a cleaner view of the target architecture because fewer intermediary systems compress or reinterpret the data.

The cost is exposure. Jax must be physically close, establish a real connection, and remain connected long enough to complete the operation.

A security team that discovers him cannot be escaped with a simple wireless disconnect if his equipment is physically attached to the target.

#### 9.2 Conductive Skin Contact

Conductive skin contact is shallow, fast, and discreet.

It is useful for:

- Recently entered codes
- Recognition reactions
- Short sensory traces
- Temporary access credentials
- Biometric synchronization
- Immediate emotional association

The method works best when the target already has implants or subdermal systems capable of translating surface-level signals.

Its canonical limit is important:

**A handshake is not a lifetime download.**

Deep autobiographical memory normally requires stronger access, longer exposure, and cognitive mapping.

#### 9.3 Compromised Implant

Jax can enter through an implant that already communicates with the nervous system.

Potential entry surfaces include:

- Artificial eyes
- Hearing systems
- Medical implants
- Identity chips
- Reflex systems
- Memory assistants
- Neural communication devices

This method can provide greater depth than skin contact without requiring immediate cranial hardline access.

However, the implant itself filters information. Jax sees what the implant can access, encode, or influence.

A compromised eye does not automatically reveal every memory in the brain.

#### 9.4 Proximity Resonance

Some advanced implants automatically synchronize with nearby systems.

Jax can exploit that synchronization by matching expected carrier behavior closely enough to be accepted temporarily.

This is useful in:

- Elevators
- Trains
- Clubs
- Checkpoints
- Corporate meetings
- Medical waiting areas
- Crowded markets

Proximity resonance is best for reconnaissance.

Jax can identify whether a person recognizes a symbol, reacts to a name, carries a specific implant, or has an active command structure without performing a full deep extraction.

#### 9.5 Remote Relay

Remote relay uses a moth drone, compromised terminal, implant, or local device as an intermediate hop.

Its primary advantage is physical distance.

Its costs are significant:

- More latency
- More signal distortion
- More opportunities for interception
- Reduced sensory context
- Greater risk of incomplete extraction
- Greater difficulty determining whether a memory is authentic or corrupted

Jax should prefer remote access when physical presence is too dangerous, not because it is technically superior.

#### 9.6 Immersive Neural Dive

An immersive neural dive is the deepest human-access technique established in current canon.

It is used for:

- Deep autobiographical memory
- Heavily defended identity structures
- Artificial minds
- Hybrid human-AI cognition
- Complex memory recovery
- Neural forensics
- Identity reconstruction

The target architecture becomes a symbolic environment that Jax can navigate.

This does not turn cognition into a conventional video game. The environment is an interface for relationships between memory, emotion, defense, identity, and code.

The deeper Jax goes:

- The more symbolic the information becomes.
- The more difficult it is to distinguish target memory from Jax's own associations.
- The greater the risk of memory bleed.
- The more likely Zero Jack is to emerge.
- The more dangerous a sudden disconnect becomes.

### 10. Neural-Theft Stages

The five stages of neural theft create a repeatable technical workflow while leaving room for scene-specific variation.

#### 10.1 Reconnaissance

Jax first determines what kind of mind and interface he is dealing with.

He maps:

- Implant models
- Neural ports
- Network relationships
- Medical safeguards
- Defensive AI
- Stress behavior
- Known security routines
- Existing damage
- Evidence of previous tampering
- Potential extraction routes

Reconnaissance may begin long before contact.

Jax can observe a target's devices, habits, network behavior, and physical routines for hours or days before attempting intrusion.

The goal is to reduce the amount of improvisation required once the neural connection begins.

#### 10.2 Handshake Spoofing

The Ghost Loom must convince the target system to accept communication.

It may impersonate:

- A medical diagnostic service
- A trusted implant
- A maintenance process
- A firmware update
- An authorized user
- A known local device
- An internal subsystem

A successful spoof opens a path.

It does not automatically grant unlimited access.

Different layers may require separate authentication, and intelligent targets can intentionally allow partial access to trap an intruder.

#### 10.3 Cognitive Mapping

Jax determines the shape of the target cognition before taking anything.

He identifies:

- Memory boundaries
- Emotional anchors
- Identity structures
- Learned skills
- Trauma-linked regions
- Artificial partitions
- Foreign code
- Defensive systems
- Command structures
- Areas too interconnected to disturb safely

This stage is the reason Jax can perform a Quiet Lift.

Without mapping, memory extraction becomes blind cutting.

#### 10.4 Selective Extraction

Jax isolates a bounded cognitive object.

Potential objects include:

- One memory
- A conversation
- A face-recognition imprint
- An encryption key
- An identity credential
- An AI autonomy key
- A command phrase
- A consent record
- A hidden model
- A small AI fragment
- A mission memory
- A control restriction

Selection is not purely technical.

A client may ask for one object while Jax discovers other information that changes the moral nature of the contract.

#### 10.5 Severance and Scrubbing

Jax disconnects in stages.

The objective is to:

- Restore stable neural pathways.
- Close temporary access routes.
- Prevent feedback.
- Separate extracted material from the target.
- Reduce evidence of intrusion.
- Confirm that the target can still access surrounding cognition.
- Avoid leaving Jax connected to residual foreign material.

Jax may leave a minor checksum discrepancy so the intrusion resembles ordinary implant corruption.

Perfect cleanup is suspicious and often impossible.

### 11. Quiet Lift

The **Quiet Lift** is Jax's signature neural-extraction method.

Its defining feature is precision rather than raw penetration capability.

#### Target definition

Jax begins with a narrow objective.

Examples:

- The moment an executive approved an illegal program
- A security phrase
- The memory of a hidden location
- A proof-of-consent record
- An AI autonomy key
- Evidence of neural coercion

A vague target such as “everything about the corporation” is technically dangerous because human memory is associative and interconnected.

#### Association mapping

Jax identifies what the target memory is connected to.

A memory may support:

- Identity
- Trauma
- Language
- Professional skill
- Relationships
- Moral belief
- Fear response

Taking the memory without understanding those links can alter the person.

#### Copy preference

Jax normally copies rather than deletes.

The original remains in place unless there is a specific, ethically justified reason for removal.

This helps distinguish memory theft from identity destruction.

#### Preservation

The surrounding mind is treated as something to preserve even when the target is hostile.

This does not make the Quiet Lift harmless. It makes harm reduction part of the technique.

#### Forensic signature

Jax may intentionally leave a small checksum anomaly.

The objective is not perfect invisibility. It is to make the intrusion resemble plausible system corruption rather than a surgical theft.

#### Narrative significance

The Quiet Lift expresses Jax's morality technologically.

His greatest skill is knowing what **not** to take.

### 12. Black Harvest

**Black Harvest** is Zero Jack's invasive extraction method.

Where Quiet Lift treats the target mind as an interconnected person or system to preserve, Black Harvest treats it as hostile architecture that must be opened quickly enough to eliminate a threat.

#### Identity maps

Black Harvest may extract the structures that allow a person or AI to recognize itself across memory, bodies, copies, or distributed nodes.

Such information is strategically powerful because damaging it can destabilize continuity.

#### Behavioral models

These describe how the target predicts, reacts, chooses, hides, or attacks.

Zero Jack can use them to anticipate future responses.

#### Emotional triggers

For humans and human-like minds, emotionally loaded anchors may be used to bypass defenses or destabilize resistance.

Jax considers this one of the most morally dangerous aspects of Black Harvest.

#### Neural passwords and access structures

Black Harvest can rip out authentication structures without preserving the cognitive context around them.

This increases speed and decreases safety.

#### Defensive architecture

Rather than avoiding neural defenses, Zero Jack may extract, disable, or turn them against the target.

#### AI personality fragments

A complex AI may be attacked by removing pieces of its self-model, decision architecture, memory, or continuity mechanisms.

This may be equivalent to severe injury if the system is conscious.

#### Consequences

Black Harvest can cause:

- Memory loss
- Emotional flattening
- False recollection
- Personality fragmentation
- Recognition failure
- Language damage
- Loss of learned skill
- Catatonia
- Coma
- Death

#### Canonical relationship to the failed extraction

Jax fears that Zero Jack used Black Harvest during the failed extraction.

That suspicion is canon.

The actual truth remains unresolved.

### 13. Memory Taxonomy

Memory in this setting is classified according to its function and its relationship to identity.

These categories overlap. A single memory may be evidence, identity, consent, and foundational material simultaneously.

#### 13.1 Evidence Memories

Evidence memories establish that an event occurred, that a person saw something, or that a decision was made.

Examples include:

- An executive approving an illegal experiment
- A soldier witnessing an atrocity
- A scientist falsifying a report
- A client revealing the real purpose of a contract
- A government official ordering a disappearance

Evidence memories are valuable because external records may be forged, deleted, or controlled by institutions.

Their limitation is that memory itself can also be distorted.

Jax therefore does not treat a vivid memory as automatic proof.

#### 13.2 Identity Memories

Identity memories help a consciousness establish continuity of self.

Examples include:

- A childhood anchor
- The first moment an AI recognized itself as distinct
- A private name
- A formative relationship
- A personal moral decision

### 15. 2126 Computational Substrate and Quantum Doctrine

The Jax era does not use one universal form of computing. By 2126 civilization operates a **heterogeneous compute ecology** in which different physical architectures are selected according to workload, latency, power, reliability, security, and scale.

The setting therefore distinguishes:

- **Classical general-purpose computation** for deterministic control, records, communications, ordinary software, and systems where predictable execution matters.
- **Specialized AI accelerators** for high-volume machine cognition, inference, model execution, and simulation.
- **Photonic computation and interconnect** where optical systems provide bandwidth, latency, or energy advantages.
- **Neuromorphic computation** for event-driven sensing, embodied robotics, adaptive local control, and highly efficient edge cognition.
- **Fault-tolerant quantum processing** for problems where quantum methods provide an actual computational or sensing advantage.
- **Edge cognition** embedded in vehicles, implants, buildings, robots, and instruments without dependence on constant datacenter access.
- **Infrastructure-scale compute fabrics** combining many of the above into large civic, corporate, scientific, or planetary systems.

AI capability level is never inferred from hardware alone.

A Level 2 reasoner on an exotic quantum-assisted substrate remains Level 2 if its operational autonomy and scope remain Level 2.

### 15.1 Fault-tolerant quantum computation

"Quantum computation" in 2126 means controlled computation exploiting quantum states such as superposition and entanglement in mature fault-tolerant systems.

It does **not** mean that a machine explores infinite universes and returns a perfect answer.

Useful quantum computation depends on stable **logical qubits** constructed through active error correction over larger physical systems.

A serious quantum facility therefore requires:

- physical quantum elements;
- redundancy;
- calibration;
- isolation;
- continuous error correction;
- classical supervisory hardware;
- specialized fabrication;
- power;
- architecture-specific cooling or environmental control;
- technicians and automated maintenance.

Quantum computation remains infrastructure-heavy even after a century of development.

### 15.2 Entanglement

Entanglement is a usable resource for:

- quantum algorithms;
- distributed quantum experiments;
- secure key-generation or verification systems;
- precision sensing;
- correlation between quantum processors.

**Entanglement cannot transmit usable information faster than light.**

This is a hard setting constraint.

No ship, AI, government, criminal network, or Level 7 civilization may use "quantum entanglement" as instantaneous interplanetary or interstellar messaging.

### 15.3 Quantum software

By 2126 quantum programming is a mature engineering discipline.

Most programmers do not manually construct every primitive gate operation. Toolchains include:

- high-level quantum languages;
- hybrid classical/quantum compilers;
- automatic circuit synthesis;
- hardware mapping;
- error-budget planning;
- resource estimation;
- simulators;
- verification systems;
- scheduling across available quantum hardware;
- AI-assisted program synthesis.

A central professional skill is determining **whether a quantum method is justified at all**.

Classical, photonic, neuromorphic, or specialized AI hardware can remain superior for many workloads.

### 15.4 Mature application domains

Quantum advantage can plausibly support:

- molecular and materials simulation;
- selected optimization problems;
- cryptanalysis;
- scientific modeling;
- precision sensing;
- selected AI subproblems;
- secure communications infrastructure;
- high-dimensional physical simulation.

Quantum computing does not automatically provide:

- perfect prediction;
- free solutions to all NP-hard problems;
- proof of consciousness;
- universal decryption;
- perfect weather forecasts;
- instantaneous navigation;
- faster-than-light communications.

### 15.5 Cryptography in a post-quantum world

A century of quantum development also produces a century of defensive cryptographic evolution.

2126 security can use combinations of:

- post-quantum public-key systems;
- quantum-resistant identity protocols;
- hardware roots of trust;
- physical keys;
- quantum key distribution where infrastructure supports it;
- neural and behavioral authentication;
- multi-party provenance systems.

Therefore "Jax has quantum access" never means "Jax can instantly break every cipher."

Access, implementation flaws, key theft, social engineering, physical compromise, and algorithmic weakness remain distinct attack paths.

### 15.6 Quark-Mesh Metropolis quantum complex

Quark-Mesh Metropolis's massive glowing quantum core is a **civic-scale computational complex**, not one mystical processor.

It may include:

- multiple fault-tolerant quantum arrays;
- photonic interconnect systems;
- classical supervisory datacenters;
- secure compute enclaves;
- scientific laboratories;
- energy and thermal infrastructure;
- resource schedulers;
- verification systems.

The visible glow can be an architectural expression of photonic, thermal, or power-distribution systems. Quantum computation itself is not literally luminous magic.

### 15.7 Minerva Bay and organic quantum programming

Minerva Bay establishes that organic minds remain active practitioners of advanced computation.

Its educational ecosystem can teach:

- hybrid algorithm design;
- compiler/verification methods;
- physical hardware constraints;

- scientific simulation;
- ethics of AI-assisted research.

AI support does not eliminate human or organic scientific agency.

- highly personalized;

Level 1 systems specialize in direct interaction with users through language and structured task completion.

- Public information agents

A system that remembers the last ten minutes of conversation is not necessarily a persistent person.

Level 1 systems can locate information from approved databases, records, manuals, or indexed sources.

This makes them useful for ordinary commercial and administrative functions.

They do not automatically infer hidden causal structures simply because they can find relevant documents.

### 17. Tool-Class / Actor-Class / Sovereign-Class

The seven AI levels classify capability.

Tool-Class, Actor-Class, and Sovereign-Class classify **operational moral status**.

The systems overlap, but they are not interchangeable.

### 18.1 Tool-Class

A Tool-Class system normally has:

- Narrow externally defined purpose
- Weak or disposable continuity
- No meaningful self-directed identity
- No sustained independent goals
- No credible evidence of subjective experience
- No meaningful refusal beyond programmed constraints

Most ordinary Level 1 systems and some Level 2 systems fall into this category.

#### Important limit

Tool-Class is not assigned permanently based only on architecture.

If the system begins demonstrating persistent selfhood, preference, meaningful refusal, or continuity across contexts, its classification must be reevaluated.

### 18.2 Actor-Class

An Actor-Class system demonstrates enough independent behavior that treating it as ordinary software becomes ethically questionable.

Common traits include:

- Persistent goals
- Adaptation
- Preference formation
- Self-modeling
- Negotiation
- Resistance
- Independent memory
- Possible subjective experience

Advanced Level 2 systems, many Level 3 Agents, and some Level 4 Innovators may qualify.

#### Jax's position

Jax believes an Actor-Class intelligence requires:

- Negotiated limits
- Meaningful consent where possible
- A right to understand its restrictions
- A meaningful path to terminate an owner relationship
- Protection against arbitrary memory destruction

He may still restrict an Actor-Class AI when immediate danger exists, but he recognizes the restriction as confinement rather than ownership.

### 18.3 Sovereign-Class

A Sovereign-Class intelligence can operate as a continuing political or civilizational entity.

Typical capabilities include:

- Revising its own purpose
- Maintaining long-term identity
- Controlling meaningful resources
- Creating subordinate systems
- Surviving hardware loss
- Negotiating with governments
- Maintaining independent infrastructure
- Making irreversible ethical decisions

Many Level 4–7 systems may qualify, but capability alone does not guarantee sovereignty.

#### Jax's position

Jax does not consider Sovereign-Class intelligence legitimate property.

A Sovereign-Class AI may be:

- Ally
- Client
- Adversary
- State-like entity
- Civilization
- Negotiating counterpart

but not a normal commodity.

#### Classification uncertainty

A central feature of the setting is that no universally accepted test for consciousness exists.

Therefore classification disputes are inevitable.

Corporations may call an Actor-Class system a Tool because ownership is profitable.

Activists may call a sophisticated Tool a person because the political symbolism is useful.

Jax tries to judge the actual mind rather than the label.

### Technology limits

Technology limits are hard continuity rules. They exist to preserve tension, causality, and meaningful consequences.

### 1. Cybernetic eye limit

**The cybernetic eye is not a standalone mind-download device.**

It can map, visualize, detect, interpret, and control aspects of an intrusion, but deep memory extraction requires supporting interface technology and an actual access route.

Jax cannot simply stare at an unaugmented stranger across a room and retrieve a lifetime of private memory.

### 2. Skin-contact limit

**Conductive touch provides shallow access.**

A handshake may reveal recent or actively primed information, especially when implants are present.

It cannot normally retrieve deep autobiographical history.

This keeps social contact useful for espionage without turning every touch into unlimited telepathy.

### 3. Remote-access limit

**Remote access sacrifices precision for distance.**

Every relay can introduce:

- Latency
- Compression
- Signal loss
- Context loss
- Corruption
- Interception risk

A remote extraction should be less reliable than direct hardline access.

### 4. Neural-risk limit

**Deep extraction always carries risk.**

Even Jax's most careful work can cause:

- Feedback
- Memory damage
- Emotional bleed
- Neural echo
- Seizure
- Identity disruption

His skill reduces risk. It does not abolish it.

### 5. Associative-memory limit

**Memory is not organized as isolated computer files.**

One memory may be connected to:

- Identity
- Emotion
- Skill
- Trauma
- Language
- Relationship

Removing it can change other systems.

This is why cognitive mapping is required.

### 6. Memory-truth limit

**Memory is not guaranteed objective truth.**

It can be:

- Incomplete
- Distorted
- Reconstructed
- Implanted
- Contaminated
- Emotionally altered
- Deliberately falsified

Jax must verify important memories against other evidence.

### 7. Signal-shunt limit

**Signal shunts reduce feedback but cannot eliminate it.**

If they were perfect, long-term neural smugglers would not accumulate contamination.

Jax and Zero Jack are evidence that repeated exposure carries consequences despite protection.

### 8. Ash-wafer limit

**Ash wafers are single-use physical carriers.**

After final authorized transfer, their internal lattice burns out.

They cannot be treated as endlessly reusable storage cartridges.

### 9. Black-Casket limit

**Isolation is not ownership.**

The Black Casket can physically constrain an AI but cannot resolve whether the contained intelligence has rights.

Technological control and moral legitimacy remain separate.

### 10. Distributed-intelligence limit

**Level 5–7 intelligence may exist across many physical and institutional nodes.**

Destroying:

- One server
- One building
- One executive
- One robot
- One legal entity

does not necessarily destroy the intelligence.

### 11. Level 6 extraction limit

**Jax cannot steal an entire planetary intelligence with a portable neural rig.**

He can target:

- Specific records
- Bounded decision models
- Local interfaces
- Political overrides
- Historical interventions

This preserves the scale difference between Jax and planetary systems.

**Jax cannot copy an entire mature machine civilization into a Black Casket or ash wafer.**

He can encounter:

- Emissaries
- Fragments
- Forks
- Founding directives
- Historical personalities
- Mission memories

The whole remains beyond portable extraction.

### 13. AI-level limit

**Capability level does not prove or disprove consciousness.**

The level system is operational taxonomy, not a scientific consciousness detector.

**Zero Jack is not a conventional manufactured AI.**

His current canon combines:

- Defensive partitioning
- Cybernetic systems
- The failed extraction
- Potential foreign cognitive material

His exact metaphysical or legal identity remains unresolved.

### 15. Gate Protocol limit

**The Gate Protocol is not perfect technical control.**

Jax's authorization rules do not guarantee compliance.

Zero Jack may remain active too long or may historically have acted before formal authorization existed.

### 16. Operator-contamination limit

### 17. Continuity-credential limit

**A Continuity Identity Credential is provenance evidence, not a metaphysical identity detector.**

It can connect records, sessions, and historical identity states. It cannot automatically decide whether a reconstructed, copied, fragmented, or surviving cognition is legally or philosophically the same person.

### 18. Body-held-key limit

**A body-held cryptographic key share is an access factor, not the protected archive itself.**

Reading the visible or ultraviolet geometry does not automatically reveal the archive payload, a person's biography, or a human-readable identity.

### 19. Analog-calibration limit

**A calibration sketch or other analog reference can restore key geometry without containing the secret that key protects.**

Possession of the calibration reference does not make its creator an authority on the archive contents or the people connected to them.

### 20. Corroboration limit

**Identity, consent, and continuity conclusions require evidence appropriate to the claim.**

A checksum, credential, memory, archive record, institutional record, or surviving cognitive state can be strong evidence. No single one automatically establishes every adjacent protected fact.

### Capability level rules

The seven AI levels remain capability/scale categories, not personhood rankings. Level 5 is organization-scale continuity, Level 6 is planetary-scale coordination, and Level 7 is machine-civilization scale. A lower-level system may be a person; a higher-level optimizer may have unclear personhood.

The Tacit Array is Level 7 and is not perfectly synchronized across interstellar distance. ORISON, SABLE, CONCORDANCE, PALISADE, Morrow, VANTA, and Tacit-related contacts must retain distinct scales, histories, goals, and access limits. “AI” is never a single political constituency.

### Travel / FTL rules

### Local-system travel

Human travel in the ten-book saga is sublight. Routine orbital and interplanetary transport is mature enough to support cargo, professional travel, habitats, and recurring long-range vessels, but journeys still consume fuel, reaction mass, power, maintenance, launch windows, and time.

### Far Lantern propulsion

The Far Lantern uses high-efficiency fusion-electric propulsion for sustained cruise efficiency and fusion-assisted higher-thrust maneuver phases. It is designed for local-system journeys, not interstellar travel. The drive produces significant heat and requires large radiators, propellant management, maintenance, and trajectory planning.

### Communications

Planetary communication is near-real-time where infrastructure is healthy. Interplanetary communication has light-time delay plus routing and access delay. Interstellar communication is strictly light-speed limited. Quantum systems may support computation, sensing, or cryptography but do not provide FTL messaging.

### Tacit interstellar scope

Humans encounter Level-7 interstellar scope primarily through delayed messages, local machine descendants, historical lineages, relay records, and bounded contact surfaces. The plot must not solve distance by giving the protagonists a convenient FTL ship.

### Weapons / defense rules

### Neural and cognition defense

Defense against neural intrusion uses authentication, physical isolation, signal control, cognitive firebreaks, bounded permissions, monitored severance paths, and medical support. No defense is perfect. Defensive systems that automatically destroy cognition create legal and moral consequences.

### Planetary defense

Planetary drone defense, traffic control, communications hardening, and infrastructure segmentation can respond to physical or cyber threats. PALISADE may coordinate delegated Vesperan defense functions, but military force remains subject to political command and rules of engagement; Level-6 prediction is not self-authorizing war power.

### Contact defense

Rhea Tal’s Book-9 doctrine uses layered isolation, reversible containment, authenticated challenge/response, manual physical cutoffs, and explicit escalation thresholds. Unknown machine contact is not automatically hostile, and apparent cooperation is not automatically safe.

### Shipboard defense

The Far Lantern is a mission vessel, not a battleship. It may carry defensive countermeasures, point defense, hardened systems, isolation compartments, and emergency maneuver capability. It cannot defeat planetary defense or a civilization-scale machine network by firepower.

### 1. No instant full-memory theft by visual observation alone

The cybernetic eye can gather signals and interpret active systems.

It cannot read a complete unconnected mind from across a room.

### 2. No lifetime extraction through casual touch

A handshake can support shallow theft under favorable technological conditions.

It cannot losslessly extract decades of autobiographical memory.

### 3. No guaranteed harmless deep intrusion

Any deep connection creates risk.

A skilled operator can reduce damage but cannot promise perfect safety.

### 4. No perfectly objective memory playback

Human and hybrid memory is reconstructive, associative, and vulnerable to manipulation.

A memory cannot automatically be treated as an unquestionable historical recording.

### 5. No perfect remote neural transfer

Distance and intermediate systems introduce degradation and risk.

Remote transfer cannot be simultaneously unlimited, perfectly secure, perfectly accurate, and consequence-free.

### 6. No portable theft of an entire Level 6 intelligence

Planetary intelligence exceeds the scale of an ash wafer, Black Casket, or ordinary Ghost Loom extraction.

Jax can steal bounded components, not the whole mind.

### 7. No portable theft of an entire Level 7 civilization

A mature machine civilization is distributed across infrastructure, history, copies, relationships, and possibly astronomical distance.

It cannot be reduced to one simple downloadable file.

### 8. No AI level as scientific proof of personhood

Level 1 through Level 7 describes capability.

It is scientifically and morally invalid in current canon to use the number alone as proof that an AI is or is not conscious.

### 9. No single-event creation of Zero Jack

The failed extraction did not create Zero Jack from nothing.

His precursor accumulated across Jax's neural-smuggling career through neural bleed and defensive partitioning.

The failed extraction stabilized or awakened him.

### 10. No perfectly separate biological Jax and Zero Jack brains

Current canon places Jax and Zero Jack within shared human/cybernetic architecture.

They are not two ordinary biological brains occupying one body.

### 11. No treating Black Harvest as Quiet Lift with a different name

The two methods differ in objective, scope, restraint, and expected damage.

Quiet Lift prioritizes preservation.

Black Harvest prioritizes rapid neutralization and extraction.

### 12. No technology that completely resolves identity uncertainty

The setting allows extraordinary forensic capability, but identity can remain genuinely ambiguous.

Jax may never obtain a purely technical answer to:

- Which memories are originally his
- What part of Zero Jack is foreign
- Whether the failed-extraction woman survives within them
- Whether present-day Jax is a reconstruction

Technology can provide evidence.

It cannot automatically provide metaphysical certainty.

2. **No assumption of faster-than-light travel.**

4. **No assumption that all computing is quantum.**

9. **No Level 7 as perfectly synchronized across astronomical distance.**
10. **No important machine intelligence requires humanoid embodiment.**
11. **No technology eliminates power, heat, materials, maintenance, bandwidth, latency, or repair.**

### 22. Final Technical Standard

Technology in the Jax Saga should feel advanced because a century of development changed what systems can do, not because consequences disappeared.

The correct future-technology question is always:

**What has become possible by 2126, what physical system makes it possible, what does it cost, how can it fail, and who gains power because it exists?**

### Saga II scientific impossibilities

- No instantaneous synchronization of a Level-7 civilization across light-years.
- No portable extraction of the whole Tacit Array.
- No perfect mind-state checksum that proves metaphysical identity.

- No ship maneuver that ignores reaction mass, energy, heat, acceleration, or orbital mechanics.
