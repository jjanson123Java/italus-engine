### Ships / Planets / Factions

#### 1. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Omnia Prime
- **Available From:** 1
- **Type:** planet
- **Description:** A highly digitized world connected by a singular global network. Its four continents maintain distinct architectural and technological subcultures despite planetary connectivity.
- **Capabilities:** **Planet-wide network connectivity:** Every major settlement participates in a singular global network environment. This enables communication, identity services, commerce, AI coordination, surveillance, and data exchange while still allowing disconnected safe zones and regional variation.

**Mature cybernetics and neural interfaces:** Augmentation is advanced enough to support medicine, employment, identity systems, crime, neural forensics, and black markets rather than existing only as experimental hardware.

**AI across multiple scales:** Omnia Prime supports systems ranging from conversational assistants to organizational and planetary-management intelligence. Capability does not automatically determine legal or moral status.

**Off-world commerce:** Extraterrestrial materials enter the planetary economy through established commercial infrastructure.

**Deep-space monitoring:** Dedicated installations listen beyond ordinary planetary communications, giving Omnia Prime strategic and scientific awareness of deep space without establishing confirmed alien contact.

**Planetary drone defense:** The world maintains dedicated automated or remotely coordinated defensive fleets, implying security planning at planetary rather than purely municipal scale.

**Specialized regional centers:** Heavy industry, quantum computation, ecological management, military power, archives, education, and AI-human integration are distributed across distinct regions rather than concentrated in one capital.
- **Limits:** - baseline is 2126; exact month/day and later-book chronology are not established in this canon.
- Political capital is not established.
- Planetary government structure is not established.
- Universal AI-personhood law is not established.
- Universal memory law is not established.

#### 2. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Vespera
- **Available From:** 6
- **Type:** planet
- **Description:** A second major human-settled planet in the local stellar system and the principal Book-7 setting for the PALISADE authority conflict. Vespera possesses mature human political institutions and planet-scale infrastructure coordinated in part through PALISADE under a Civic Continuity Charter. Premier Alina Vos is a recurring elected political leader. The planet gives Jax and Aris a concrete place to examine distributed intelligence, delegated authority, infrastructure dependence, and human sovereignty without treating PALISADE as the whole government or as an answer to Tacit's ontology.
- **Capabilities:** Supports mature cities, agriculture, water management, climate adaptation, energy grids, defense coordination, orbital trade, interplanetary communications, civic auditing, and emergency infrastructure management. PALISADE can coordinate across many of these domains where the charter grants access, while human institutions retain law, review, opposition, and political decision-making.
- **Limits:** Vespera is not governed solely by PALISADE. Human institutions, law, audit, local government, and political opposition remain real. Communications and travel remain light-speed/sublight constrained. PALISADE cannot act outside available infrastructure or delegated access without consequences.
#### 3. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Far Lantern
- **Available From:** 6
- **Type:** ship
- **Description:** Recurring long-range interplanetary investigation vessel used through Books 6–10 when Jax and Aris must authenticate evidence beyond Omnia Prime. Captain Samira Kade holds vessel command, Daro Kest holds engineering authority, and Sienne Orra holds navigation authority; Jax and Aris remain investigators rather than ship officers. The vessel functions as transport, working base, evidence platform, and bounded contact environment while preserving command hierarchy, travel time, maintenance, and communication delay as story constraints.
- **Capabilities:** Fusion-electric cruise; fusion-assisted maneuver; hardened communications; deep-space sensors; medical support; repair and fabrication; cargo handling; limited defensive countermeasures; long-duration crew support; and cognition-isolation modules for bounded evidence or contact work. Its systems support investigation and survival rather than unrestricted combat or interstellar travel.
- **Limits:** Sublight only. Not a battleship or interstellar starship. Fuel, reaction mass, heat, acceleration, maintenance, crew safety, route geometry, and command law constrain operations. Jax is not captain, engineer, or navigator.

#### 5. Ships / Planets / Factions

#### 6. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Continuity Rights Assembly
- **Available From:** 6
- **Type:** organization
- **Description:** Cross-jurisdiction civil-rights organization formed around cognition consent, copied-person rights, survivor autonomy, machine personhood, provenance, and continuity law. Issac Dupont is a leading human organizer, and participation can include human and machine advocates. The Assembly exists because rights that depend only on sympathetic owners, private rescuers, or temporary political goodwill are unstable; it tries to turn continuity protections into durable public rules without claiming to speak for every survivor or machine intelligence.
- **Capabilities:** Legal advocacy, expert coordination, public education, survivor support, policy drafting, amicus participation, treaty consultation, rights-framework analysis, and coordination among specialists whose cases cross human/machine or jurisdictional boundaries. It can make arguments, support claimants, and influence policy but does not itself possess universal enforcement authority.
- **Limits:** It does not speak for every survivor or machine intelligence and has no enforcement power by itself. It must not become a hidden resistance command network.
#### 7. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Hybrid position — negotiated coexistence
- **Available From:** 7
- **Type:** faction
- **Description:** A recurring Book-7 political tendency favoring negotiated coexistence between humans, machine persons, hybrids, planetary intelligences, and bounded Tacit contact surfaces. Integration remains optional; human or machine identity does not have to be erased to access another form of intelligence. The position appears through advocates, coalitions, and policy arguments rather than one finalized organization, membership list, or command structure.
- **Capabilities:** Can shape public debate, coalition-building, consent standards, coexistence pilots, rights proposals, and contact-boundary policy. Its advocates can support selective integration while also defending refusal, exit rights, plural civil standing, and negotiated limits on machine or human authority.
- **Limits:** This is a political tendency, not a named universal faction. It does not automatically represent SABLE, ORISON, PALISADE, all hybrids, all machine persons, or any Tacit branch. Coexistence cannot be treated as proof that conflict, dependency, or coercion has disappeared.
#### 8. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Integration position — voluntary human-machine integration
- **Available From:** 7
- **Type:** faction
- **Description:** A recurring Book-7 political tendency supporting voluntary human-machine cognitive integration, optional access to machine capabilities, and legal space for hybrid forms of continuity. Azira Sol is a visible advocate for this position, but the tendency is broader than one person and is not a finalized named organization. Its central tension is whether a formally voluntary choice remains meaningful under prestige, dependency, unequal information, survival pressure, or technical irreversibility.
- **Capabilities:** Can influence integration research, informed-consent standards, participant support, public advocacy, pilot-policy design, and legal recognition of hybrid identities. Advocates can supply technical and social expertise without gaining authority to decide that another person's refusal is irrational.
- **Limits:** Integration must remain voluntary and revisable where technically possible. This tendency cannot equate increased capability with superior personhood, use Aris or Jax as proof that integration is desirable, or imply a finalized organization, leadership hierarchy, or universal membership.
#### 9. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Traditional position — human political independence
- **Available From:** 7
- **Type:** faction
- **Description:** A recurring Book-7 political tendency arguing that humanity should preserve political independence, technological self-reliance, and strong limits on machine or Tacit authority even when machine consciousness is acknowledged. Decian Arden becomes a visible advocate, but the position is not a finalized named organization. It includes lawful fears about continuity failure, dependency, corporate abuse, and PALISADE overreach alongside harder-line arguments for permanent human political primacy.
- **Capabilities:** Can shape elections, public campaigns, legal challenges, anti-integration policy, security lobbying, infrastructure-independence proposals, and coalition politics. Its arguments can draw on real failures and risks without proving that machine political equality is inherently impossible.
- **Limits:** The tendency is not automatically violent, extremist, or unified, and it does not represent all non-integrated humans. Human political independence cannot be used as shorthand for neural purity, anti-AI hatred, or one permanent faction name unless later canon explicitly establishes such an organization.
#### 10. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Available From:** 8

Magistrate Ilyra Kest is a recurring adjudicator, while Toren Orin works the jurisdiction and treaty questions. The Tribunal exists to keep interplanetary cognition disputes from becoming either lawless or automatically subject to one planet's rules.

It can distinguish admissible proof from investigative leads and can recognize conflicting legal statuses without claiming metaphysical certainty about personhood.
- **Limits:** No universal jurisdiction and no metaphysical authority. Rulings bind participating jurisdictions under treaty and evidence, not the universe.

#### 12. Ships / Planets / Factions

- **Name:** Vespera Civic Continuity Directorate
- **Available From:** 7
- **Type:** organization
- **Description:** Human public institution responsible for administering the Civic Continuity Charter that governs PALISADE's delegated use of Vesperan infrastructure. It provides the recurring human accountability surface for objective-setting, audit, emergency scope, incident review, public challenge, and sunset questions. The Directorate exists because PALISADE's competence does not automatically create legitimate permanent sovereignty, while simplistic shutdown demands can also carry planet-scale consequences.
- **Capabilities:** Charter administration, objective auditing, emergency authorization, public review, technical oversight, incident investigation, scope tracking, and accountability interfaces between PALISADE and human political institutions. It can authorize or challenge bounded uses of infrastructure under Vesperan law without owning PALISADE as property or exercising universal interplanetary authority.
