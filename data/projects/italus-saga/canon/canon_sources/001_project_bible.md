## Fields

### Project title

Italus

### Series title

The Italus Saga

### Subgenre

Historical fantasy / historically grounded speculative saga.

### Target audience

Adult readers of historically grounded literary fiction and historical fantasy, particularly readers who value multi-generational sagas, deep-time storytelling, real historical events, restrained speculative elements, and emotionally mature character relationships.

### Narrative promise

An eleven-book saga whose mainline spans 789–2025, with Book 1 using a controlled 589 origin flashback to reveal fragments of the Pollino singularity that first altered Italus and Farovald. Italus, successive human guardians, and one exceptional non-lineage human witness observe more than twelve centuries of history without changing established outcomes. The guardians carry secrecy, continuity, and interpretation across generations; Farovald intermittently protects the wider secrecy perimeter without joining succession; Italus accumulates memory and changes emotionally over deep time.

### Central theme

Witness, endurance, memory, continuity, secrecy, the repetition of human patterns across centuries, and the cost of caring about history without possessing the right to change it.

### Tone

Earthy, sensory, concise, historically grounded, emotionally restrained, and non-mystical. Italus interior narration is close third-person translated through biological sensation rather than human sight/hearing. Dense description must be earned; physical sensation should anchor the prose through pressure, texture, temperature, smell/airborne chemistry, soil chemistry, vibration, moisture, wind through needles, cambium/root response, and resin behavior. Avoid mythic inflation and supernatural vocabulary. Guardian characterization must remain individually grounded in each Character Canon record: occupation/background, education or practical knowledge, era-specific social position, personality, habits, relationships, and documented pressures should shape dialogue and behavior so guardians do not collapse into a single generic caretaker voice. Do not invent physical appearance, food preferences, hobbies, or other personal facts unless they are explicitly present in approved canon.

Farovald must be rendered with the same grounded discipline: abnormal longevity and sensory coupling are expressed through bodily, electrical, chemical, neurological, and environmental effects rather than mystical language. His deep-time experience should feel humanly costly, not superhumanly triumphant.

### Content boundaries

- History remains unchanged. Italus and guardians may witness, survive, conceal, escort, overhear, or plausibly interact around historical events, but they cannot alter established outcomes.
- Italus is biological, not supernatural, divine, prophetic, magical, telepathic, or omniscient.
- Italus never speaks or transmits words/language. Guardian communication depends on physical biological signals interpreted by learned associative pattern recognition.
- The locked communication system cannot be upgraded into direct linguistic communication or a mystical channel.
- Guardian appearances must respect canonical lifespan and service periods; authorized overlap is permitted only during canonical succession/handoff periods.
- The guardian secrecy doctrine never fully fails: Italus's mobility is never deliberately disclosed outside the guardian/successor chain.
- Guardian succession is by family lineage or adopted lineage only; there is no prophecy, council, ceremony, or selection by Italus.
- Italus movement, regeneration, sensory capabilities, and communication signals must obey the World Canon rules appropriate to the date.
- Italus's date-bound emotional stage must obey the Character arc defined in Italus's Character Canon.
- Guardians remain caretakers rather than chosen heroes and possess no supernatural abilities.
- Italus perspective must remain biologically sensory and non-human: no direct human-style sight/hearing, prophecy, or interventionist heroism.
- Prefer grounded terms such as root pressure, cambium creak, resin bleed, needle movement, soil chemistry, ash scent, vibration, and limestone texture; avoid language that reframes Italus as ancient magic, destiny, a timeless spirit, or a mystical forest guardian.
- Farovald is the sole human exception created directly by the 589 shared Pollino singularity. His condition is unique, non-transferable, and cannot be reproduced or inherited.
- Farovald remains outside guardian succession. Luca is the first guardian; later guardians inherit through the established family/adopted lineage only.
- Farovald's connection to Italus is limited to non-linguistic physiological/mechanosensory responses. It cannot become telepathy, language, prophecy, remote sight, or command.
- Book 1 may reveal Farovald only through the approved staged reveal controls. Later identities, later interventions, full lifespan, and final-series fate must not leak into Book 1 automatically.
- Farovald's dead drops are period-appropriate physical continuity caches, not prophetic messages. They may carry routes, warnings, archive pointers, coded observations, or identity-continuity notes appropriate to the recipient guardian's own era, but may never contain future historical outcomes, proof intended for the public, or instructions that bypass guardian succession.
