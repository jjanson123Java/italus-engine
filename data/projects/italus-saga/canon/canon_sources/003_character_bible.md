#### 1. Characters

- **Name:** Italus
- **Aliases:** Heldreich's pine; `Pinus heldreichii` (Bosnian pine).
- **Available From:** 1
- **Narrative role:** Central witness and continuity character across the eleven-book saga. Italus is a biologically anomalous mobile tree whose accumulated memory, sensory experience, and changing relationship with successive guardians form the saga's non-human continuity axis.
- **Status:** active
- **Age or lifespan:** Already mature by 589 AD; exact germination date is unknown. The 589 Pollino lightning singularity begins Italus's anomalous mobility. Italus survives through the modern endpoint of the saga.
- **First appearance:** Book 1 — 789 AD mainline, Pollino Mountains. Book 1 may briefly look back to the 589 Pollino singularity through controlled external analepsis.
- **Relationships:** The guardian bond is mutual but non-linguistic. Guardians protect, route, conceal, tend, and interpret Italus; Italus provides continuity, recognition, extended biological exposure, and a stable nonjudgmental presence. Communication occurs through the locked physical signal system defined in World Canon and through guardian-specific learned patterns. Luca establishes the earliest guardian touch correspondence; Elia systematizes observation; later guardians inherit and extend the tradition without converting it into spoken/direct language.

Farovald is the sole human directly altered during the same 589 Pollino singularity that gives Italus mobility. The connection does not permit language, thought transfer, prophecy, or command. Farovald can experience limited bodily responses correlated with major root pressure, resin temperature, electrical irritation, cambium stress, and related physical changes. This is mechanosensory coupling, not telepathy. Farovald remains outside the guardian succession.
- **Goals:** Persist, remain biologically viable, return to Pollino soil as required, maintain bounded relationships with guardians, and survive historical change without becoming an agent that alters human history. Italus has no conventional heroic quest.
- **Fears:** Canonically relevant pressures include exposure/destruction, loss of guardians, unsuitable soil, failure to regenerate in Pollino substrate, prolonged separation from the guardian lineage, and eventually finishing its life alone. These should remain non-human in expression rather than conventional melodramatic fear.
- **Voice:** Close third-person interior translated through non-human biological sensation. Observant, patient, rarely surprised, occasionally sad through accumulated memory, compressed, sensory, time-aware, and explicitly non-mystical. Italus does not see/hear as a human; perception is rendered through vibration, pressure, wind/needle response, moisture, soil chemistry, bark temperature, airborne chemistry, and related physical cues.
- **Character arc:** Pre-guardian adaptation — 589–789 AD: Italus adapts to anomalous mobility in concealed Pollino terrain while Farovald is the only human who knows the condition exists. Stage 1 — Curiosity: 789–989 AD. Stage 2 — Observation: 989–1389 AD. Stage 3 — Detachment: 1389–1789 AD. Stage 4 — Weariness: 1789–present. The 589–789 adaptation interval does not move or renumber the established guardian-era emotional stages. Core traits endure; emotional register changes slowly. Stage boundaries/names are protected canon and Italus does not regress or skip stages.
- **Canon constraints:** - Italus never speaks, transmits words, uses telepathy, or communicates through a mystical channel.
- Physical signals are biological responses interpreted through experience and pattern recognition; they are not a direct language.
- World Canon's full signal matrix and sensory limits control all guardian–Italus interactions.
- Farovald's 589 coupling is limited to non-linguistic bodily responses correlated with physical tree/root-network conditions; it does not grant words, images, memories, prophecy, remote omniscience, or command over Italus.
- Italus cannot alter historical events or become a heroic savior.
- Movement/regeneration must obey World Canon biological limits.
- Emotional stage must match the date; 589–789 is a pre-guardian adaptation interval and Stage 1 begins with Luca's 789 discovery.
- Italus does not forget a guardian.
- No later-developed signal interpretation may leak backward into an earlier guardian era.
- Italus is already mature by 589; canon must not describe 789 as its germination or biological birth.
#### 2. Characters

- **Name:** Luca
- **Aliases:** Not separately specified in legacy canon.
- **Available From:** 1
- **Narrative role:** First guardian and founder of the guardian lineage. Before guardianship Luca is a Lucanian shepherd boy from the southern mountain country, approximately fourteen years old when he discovers evidence of Italus's movement in 789. His practical knowledge is rooted in animals, weather, mountain terrain, shelter, seasonal movement, and patient observation rather than formal education or theology. Farovald may advise or test him, but Luca remains the first guardian and the founder of the lineage's practical secrecy methods.
- **Status:** dead
- **Age or lifespan:** Born: ~775 AD; Died: ~900 AD; approximate lifespan: ~125 years.
- **First appearance:** Book 1 — 789 AD, when Luca discovers fresh ground and root displacement showing that Italus has moved.
- **Relationships:** The dynamic in the first years is primarily observational: Luca studies Italus and Italus gradually becomes accustomed to Luca. There is no shared language and no established code. By roughly Luca's fortieth year, the two develop an unwritten touch correspondence through repeated proximity and pattern recognition — a hand on bark meaning stay, a specific knock meaning move at nightfall, and body weight settling against the trunk meaning rest / we are safe. Elia later reconstructs this practice from observing them together.

**Farovald — recurring non-lineage adviser.** Farovald watches Luca's repeated 789 visits from concealment and later intercepts him on a mountain route while presenting himself only as a reclusive monk. He recognizes that Luca's excuses are attracting attention and advises him to use prayer as a socially credible reason for repeated trips into the heights. Farovald protects the wider perimeter and occasionally tests Luca's caution, but does not appoint Luca, does not belong to the guardian succession, and does not replace Luca's authorship of the lineage's practical methods.

**Family / lineage relationships**
- **Elia — daughter and eventual successor.** Elia grows up observing Luca's second life beside Italus and learns by inclusion rather than a formal announcement. Her eventual knowledge is legitimate lineage succession, not accidental public disclosure.
- **Other family members — not individually canonized.** Do not infer that Luca's other relatives knew Italus could move or possessed guardian knowledge.
- One-scene outsiders remain Event Ledger characters unless later promoted by author-approved canon.
- **Goals:** Keep Italus alive and undisclosed; learn what the tree needs through observation rather than explanation; find safe shelter and routes in the Lucanian/Pollino landscape; and preserve the responsibility long enough for a successor to understand it. Luca's approach is practical: ask what Italus needs, notice physical patterns, and choose silence over disclosure.
- **Fears:** No explicit personal fear list survives. Canonical pressures include discovery of Italus, inability to understand or meet the tree's physical needs, public suspicion caused by Luca's unusually slow aging, and the danger that no one after him will understand the unwritten responsibility.
- **Voice:** Practical patience; low verbal expression; deep observation; instinctive loyalty. Luca is not scholarly or rhetorically ornate. He notices terrain, weather, animals, physical needs, and behavior before theory. His speech should be economical, concrete, and question-driven rather than philosophical.
- **Character arc:** A shepherd boy from the Lucanian hills, approximately fourteen years old when he discovers fresh displacement around Italus in 789. He has no framework for a moving tree, so he responds first with attention: he returns, compares position and ground disturbance, and begins concealing the evidence. His increasingly weak excuses draw family and village suspicion. Farovald, appearing only as a mountain monk, gives one practical piece of advice — use prayer as the cover for repeated trips — but Luca develops the larger guardian practice through decades of tending, observation, route choice, concealment, and responsibility. As his peers age and he ages unusually slowly under long-term resin exposure, Luca adopts changing identities and avoids sustained scrutiny. His lifespan remains approximately 125 years because his exposure is partial compared with later guardians. He dies in the mountains in autumn after establishing a viable handoff to Elia.

Canonical guardian service arc: 789–900 AD. Luca is the first guardian. Farovald is never a guardian and never becomes part of succession.

#### 6. Characters

- **Aliases:** Brother Anselm; Anselm

- **Status:** dead

Italus registered his devotion as pattern rather than meaning: the same posture, the same sequence, the same return. Over decades Italus came to find this regularity stabilising. Anselm, for his part, wrote that the tree was the closest thing to a confessor he had found — it received everything and disclosed nothing.

#### 8. Characters

- **Name:** Amara of Palermo
- **Aliases:** Not separately specified in legacy canon.
- **Available From:** 4
- **Narrative role:** Seventh guardian; adopted-line Sicilian woman of Arab descent who works as a translator and document secretary in Frederick II's Palermo court before and during her recruitment into the guardian network. Her linguistic skill, court experience, political acuity, and document access make her the most politically sophisticated medieval guardian and an expert in travel papers, route intelligence, cover documentation, and identity management.
- **Status:** dead
- **Age or lifespan:** Born: ~1195 AD; Died: ~1310 AD; approximate lifespan: ~115 years.
- **First appearance:** First canonical Event Ledger appearance: **Book 4, 1220, LEGACY-EVENT-053**.
- **Relationships:** Amara's relationship with Italus was professional first and personal second — a reversal of most guardian dynamics. She was the first guardian to approach the tree as a long-term strategic problem rather than a daily caretaking responsibility, and this gave their interaction a quality of mutual assessment that earlier relationships lacked. Italus in Stage 2 had begun developing its observational detachment, and Amara's consistent preference for analysis over sentiment was compatible with this. She did not lean against the tree and weep. She sat at a measured distance and thought. Italus found this respectful. Over time, the measured distance closed.
