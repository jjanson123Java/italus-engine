## Fields

### Historical period

589–2025 AD for the saga's anomaly chronology, with Farovald's c.550 birth as pre-anomaly backstory and 789 AD as the mainline narrative opening. Medieval southern Italy through the modern era remains the principal historical span.

### Setting summary

This is a historical-fiction saga on real Earth. Italus is a mature Heldreich's pine (`Pinus heldreichii`) already established in the Pollino Mountains by 589 AD; its exact germination date is unknown. In 589 a direct lightning event passing through Italus, the mineral-rich limestone substrate, and an unusual mycorrhizal network produces the saga's single shared biological singularity. The event restructures Italus's root/cambium physiology enough to permit slow physical mobility and simultaneously leaves Farovald — a nearby human in direct contact with the struck tree — with a unique, radically slowed-aging physiological anomaly. For roughly two centuries Italus and Farovald remain concealed in the Pollino heights. In 789 Luca discovers fresh physical evidence that Italus can move and becomes the first guardian. The guardian lineage then carries practical tending, concealment, route management, and succession through 2025. Farovald remains outside that lineage and intermittently protects the wider secrecy perimeter. Italus, Farovald, and the guardians witness history; they do not alter it.

**Locked biological origin of mobility:** the 589 mobility change depends on the same three interacting physical conditions: (1) direct lightning damage/restructuring of mature root and cambium tissue, (2) mineral-rich limestone substrate contributing unusual tensile/root chemistry conditions, and (3) the local mycorrhizal network functioning as a slow electrochemical conductor. Farovald's survival anomaly requires his direct physical contact with the damaged tree during that same event and is non-repeatable. No supernatural explanation may replace or supplement these mechanisms.

### Geography

Primary anchor: Pollino Mountains at the Basilicata/Calabria border in southern Italy, approximately 2,000 meters elevation. The saga expands through southern and central Italian corridors, Sicily, Rome-adjacent routes, selected Mediterranean/European corridors, and later modern Italian settings.

Movement is geographically constrained. Italus has traveled as far as the Iberian Peninsula and the Levant but must return to Pollino soil. Journeys beyond roughly 800 miles one-way require multi-year recovery cycles. Italus has never entered a major city; city-based historical encounters occur at edges, surrounding villages, transit camps, gardens, roads, or countryside where concealment and rooting remain possible.

### Cultures and societies

Historically grounded societies appropriate to each era, including Byzantine/Lombard/Frankish southern Italy, Arab and Norman Sicily, monastic and papal networks, Renaissance and early-modern Italy, Enlightenment intellectual culture, the Risorgimento and unified Italian state, wartime and fascist Italy, postwar republican Italy, and modern European society. Guardian behavior, cover identities, literacy, records, travel, social roles, and historical contacts must remain plausible for the active period.

### Political rules

- Political power, borders, institutions, wars, successions, and historical outcomes follow documented history.
- Guardians may use institutions, patronage, monasteries, legal identities, travel papers, or cover stories when historically plausible, but cannot cause or prevent canonical historical outcomes.
- Guardians develop structural skepticism toward institutional power and use it pragmatically rather than treating institutions as inherently authoritative.
- The secrecy doctrine overrides political loyalty: protecting Italus's secret is the guardian's fixed obligation.
- Cover identities and identity cycling must remain plausible for the administrative technology of the era.

### Religious / mythic context

Religious institutions, beliefs, schisms, papal politics, monasteries, crusading culture, and local faith practices are historically real features of the setting. Characters may interpret Italus through period-appropriate beliefs, but canon itself never validates a supernatural explanation. Italus is not divine, prophetic, magical, a spirit, a chosen guardian, or an agent of destiny.

### Historical constraints

### Historical immutability
- Historical events occur on their established dates/date ranges and retain their documented outcomes.
- Historical figures must be alive and geographically plausible.
- Fictional characters may observe, overhear, escort, conceal, survive, receive reports, or have bounded plausible interaction without changing recorded history.

### Canon authority for historical participation
- World Canon defines the global physical and historical limits on fictional participation in real history.
- The **Timeline & Historical Event Ledger** governs what fictional participation is permitted or required for a specific historical event.
- The **Historical Character Interaction Map** governs the permitted scope of contact with a named historical figure.
- An Event Ledger permission does not create unrestricted access to a named historical figure. If a named historical figure is involved, the scene must also conform to the Historical Character Interaction Map.
- Event and interaction records may narrow World Canon permissions for a specific scene, event, or person, but may never relax, override, or contradict World Canon historical immutability, secrecy, biology, geography, or guardian-timeline rules.
- Historical events may influence Italus or a guardian indirectly through reports, rumor, documents, travelers, political consequences, or regional effects. An Event Ledger record does not by itself establish Italus's physical presence at the event location.

### Guardian timeline and succession
- Guardian service periods are canonical and must match scene dates.
- Multiple guardians may coexist only during canonical succession/handoff overlaps; overlap is not itself a continuity error.
- Guardian succession has exactly two canonical methods: family lineage (primary) and adopted lineage (secondary). Italus does not choose guardians; there is no prophecy, council, supernatural selection, or mandatory ceremony.
- Guardian knowledge at any date is limited to what the lineage could have transmitted or discovered by that date.

### Secrecy doctrine
- Italus's mobility must not be deliberately disclosed to the wider world.
- Primary concealment methods include night travel, temporary planting among comparable trees, bounded use of specimen-under-transport cover stories when necessary, routing around major cities, and staged explanation/misdirection/relocation when curiosity becomes dangerous.
- No guardian deliberately discloses Italus to anyone except a legitimate prospective successor.

### Guardian knowledge tradition
- Canon knowledge is distributed across oral transmission, coded botanical notebooks, personal journals, hidden maps of safe forests/routes, and orally preserved procedures.
- Botanical notebooks begin with Brother Anselm around 1054 AD; encoded movement/routes/concealment/resin effects are hidden inside apparently botanical material.
- Hidden route maps track safe forest stands, water/mineral sources, dangerous zones, and seasonal timing.
- Annual regeneration procedure, root-care protocol, and emergency concealment steps remain oral rather than written.
- No single historical record is complete; the full knowledge exists across the lineage.

### Communication chronology
- Italus–guardian communication is learned through years of proximity and associative pattern recognition, not translation.
- Signals are normally interpreted as clusters, not as one-to-one words.
- A guardian cannot use a signal interpretation or systematic method before it has been discovered/transmitted in the lineage.
- Luca's early touch correspondence is intuitive and unwritten; Elia systematizes physical-state observation; later guardians inherit and extend the observational tradition without converting it into language.

### Farovald dead drops and historical immutability
- Farovald dead drops are private fictional continuity events. They cannot cause or alter a documented historical outcome.
- A cache may be hidden in a real historical setting only when its placement and recovery are geographically and materially plausible for the date.
- No named historical person may knowingly receive or authenticate a Farovald cache unless a compatible Historical Interaction Map record explicitly permits that contact.

### Fictional allowances

### Italus movement and regeneration
- Speed: approximately 1–2 miles per day on flat terrain; roughly half that on slopes.
- Mechanism: roots act as tendons/grappling anchors; trunk lean and rolling anchor placement allow slow relocation before re-rooting.
- Movement consumes stored photosynthate sugars. Extended travel can cause pale needles, reduced resin output, and brittle bark at root/joint areas.
- Italus must spend part of each year rooted in Pollino soil so the specific fungal symbiont/substrate can restore electrochemical tissue. Without this, mobility degrades over years and may cease permanently.

### Italus sensory capabilities
Italus does not see or hear as a human does. Canon-compatible sensory channels include:
- soil vibration/root-tip mechanoreception: footsteps, horses, carts, troop movement, ground disturbance;
- wind patterns through needles, including directional airflow and airborne scent;
- moisture/humidity and atmospheric-pressure changes;
- soil/root-network chemistry, including ash, iron-heavy soil/blood, nitrogen/decomposition, phosphate/disturbed earth, and related chemical shifts;
- thermal awareness through bark, including sun, fire, and human body heat;
- airborne chemistry through needle surfaces, including smoke, decomposition, crowd/animal odors, disease-related environmental cues, and foreign plant resins.

### Italus–Guardian Communication System — locked canon
The signals of Italus are **not a language**. Guardians interpret biological responses through years of observation, physical proximity, and pattern recognition.

Observed signals include:
- **resin output and variation**;
- **needle tension**;
- **root pressure**;
- **bark / physical condition**;
- **responses correlated with weather, soil conditions, and human proximity**;
- trunk lean/position;
- resin temperature and placement;
- soil vibration;
- root-network chemistry;
- root disturbance and root-surface heat;
- learned touch/knock correspondence established between guardians and Italus.

Signal clusters may convey practical contextual meaning. Canon examples include root pressure + warm resin indicating approaching humans, and needle vibration + trunk lean indicating weather change plus directional suggestion.

### Canonical signal interpretation matrix
**Root pressure**
- RP-1 shallow root pulses → move soon
- RP-2 deep pressure waves → urgent relocation
- RP-3 repeated root tightening → danger nearby
- RP-4 root withdrawal pattern → avoid current ground
- RP-5 lateral root pull → change direction
- RP-6 sudden root stillness → remain hidden

**Trunk position**
- TL-1 slight north lean → preferred travel direction
- TL-2 rapid lean correction → terrain instability
- TL-3 slow leaning hold → pause movement
- TL-4 full trunk tension → energy exhaustion
- TL-5 repeated sway → environmental disturbance

**Resin**
- RS-1 warm resin flow → approaching humans
- RS-2 cool resin secretion → safe environment
- RS-3 heavy resin bleed → injury or root stress
- RS-4 intermittent resin secretion → weather change
- RS-5 resin concentration near root seam → need Pollino soil regeneration
- RS-6 resin concentration on south bark → heat or fire risk

**Needle movement**
- ND-1 synchronized needle tremor → wind shift
- ND-2 localized crown vibration → approaching animals
- ND-3 irregular crown movement → storm pressure
- ND-4 needle tension → drought stress
- ND-5 crown droop → prolonged exhaustion

**Soil chemistry**
- SC-1 iron concentration spike → blood nearby
- SC-2 nitrogen surge → decomposition
- SC-3 ash trace in soil → fire aftermath
- SC-4 sulphur change → volcanic or burned terrain
- SC-5 disturbed phosphate layer → burial or digging

**Root-network sensory signals**
- RN-1 vibration clusters → marching soldiers
- RN-2 irregular vibration → cavalry movement
- RN-3 heavy rhythmic ground pressure → wagon/cart traffic
- RN-4 faint tremor waves → earthquake precursor

### Guardian longevity and resin exposure
- Long-term guardian proximity and annual root-care expose guardians to diluted resin compounds through skin absorption and inhalation.
- The effect is biological: slowed cellular aging and enhanced healing; sustained guardians may live roughly 100–200 years, with late-entry/partial-exposure guardians showing reduced effects.
- Exposure accumulates over years/decades, not a single encounter.
- Separation allows effects to fade as compounds metabolize; longevity is not permanently retained after abandonment/separation.
- Guardians remain vulnerable to violence, serious infection, trauma, accident, and toxic misuse of concentrated resin.
- Concentrated/preserved resin is not a safe transferable longevity treatment; attempts at misuse have produced toxicity.
- The longevity effect must never be presented as a mystical gift, reward, blessing, or supernatural exchange.

### Farovald — sole 589 human exception
- Farovald is born approximately 550 AD and is about thirty-nine when the 589 shared singularity occurs.
- His longevity is distinct from normal guardian resin exposure: it begins in the single 589 event, is radically stronger, and cannot be reproduced, transferred, inherited, harvested, or induced in another person.
- Farovald may experience limited bodily responses correlated with major Italus/root-network conditions: pressure-like nerve responses, resin-temperature changes, electrical irritation, cambium/root stress, and large ground-pressure disturbances.
- These responses are not language. They cannot contain words, sentences, images, memories, intent, prophecy, or remote sensory vision.
- Farovald remains vulnerable to trauma, illness, exhaustion, disorientation, and physiological degradation. Longevity does not equal invulnerability.
- Farovald does not belong to guardian succession and cannot appoint, select, or replace a guardian. Luca remains the first guardian.
- Farovald's abnormal lifespan must remain secret; exposure of his unaging identity is treated as a threat to the Secrecy Doctrine.
- Later cover identities and later-series disclosures require author-controlled reveal planning; they are not automatically available to Book 1 prose.

### Farovald dead-drop / continuity-cache protocol — locked canon
- Farovald may use a dead drop only to support an active guardian or an already-authorized successor when direct contact would create avoidable exposure.
- Every dead drop is a physical, period-appropriate object using materials and storage methods that plausibly exist in the date of the event.
- Permitted content is limited to information Farovald could have learned by that date: current/past route conditions, concealment warnings, archive pointers, observations, continuity checks, and identity-management cautions.
- A dead drop may not contain prophecy, fixed future outcomes, knowledge from a later century, a complete account of Farovald's lifespan, or instructions that make Farovald a succession authority.
- Authentication must use a two-part lineage-compatible method: a concealed site marker plus an internal cross-reference to knowledge already possessed by the active guardian. No dead drop may contain a self-authenticating public proof of Italus, guardian longevity, or Farovald's abnormal lifespan.
- Farovald does not use Italus as a message carrier and does not cut, scar, or damage Italus to create a cache.
- Caches can be lost, damaged, discovered late, or recovered only in part. They do not automatically repair every break in the guardian record.
- A dead drop cannot replace an explicitly destroyed or missing historical guardian record unless the Timeline Ledger states exactly which bounded information survived in the cache.
- Specific dead drops are canonical only when recorded in the Timeline Event Ledger. Their discovery or authorship may still be staged through Planner Reveal controls at the appropriate book/chapter.

### Forbidden contradictions

- Italus speaking, telepathically communicating, transmitting words/sentences, or using any direct linguistic or mystical communication channel.
- Treating individual signals as a fully translatable vocabulary independent of guardian experience/context; signals are biological patterns normally interpreted in clusters.
- Replacing, upgrading, or contradicting the canonical physical signal system.
- Omitting or contradicting the observed signal channels: resin output/variation, needle tension, root pressure, bark/physical condition, and correlations with weather, soil conditions, and human proximity.
- Italus seeing/hearing as a human, becoming omniscient, prophesying, or knowing fixed future outcomes.
- Italus behaving as supernatural, magical, divine, mythic, or a heroic savior.
- Moving Italus's mobility origin away from the locked 589 shared singularity, describing 789 as Italus's germination/birth, or substituting a supernatural origin.
- Movement faster than canonical biological limits, movement without energy cost, or long-range travel without realistic recovery/regeneration.
- Permanent mobility without periodic Pollino regeneration.
- Italus or a guardian changing a documented historical event/outcome.
- A guardian acting outside canonical lifespan/service dates.
- Multiple active guardians together outside an authorized transition/handoff.
- A succession method other than family or adopted lineage without explicit canon amendment.
- Italus selecting a guardian through prophecy, destiny, or supernatural preference.
- A historical figure appearing outside lifespan or plausible geography.
- Deliberate public disclosure of Italus's mobility or complete collapse of the secrecy doctrine.
- Treating guardian longevity as magical, instantaneous, permanent after separation, or safely transferable through concentrated resin.
- Emotional-stage behavior, signal knowledge, or communication interpretation that belongs to a later era leaking backward into an earlier one.
- Treating Farovald as a guardian, Guardian Zero, a succession authority, or the founder of the guardian lineage.
- Treating Farovald's longevity as ordinary guardian resin exposure, as transferable, reproducible, hereditary, or magical.
- Giving Farovald telepathy, direct linguistic communication with Italus, remote sight/hearing through Italus, prophecy, or omniscience.
- Allowing later Farovald aliases, later interventions, final lifespan, or final-series fate to leak into Book 1 without an author-selected reveal control.
- Treating a Farovald dead drop as prophecy, a message from the future, a magically updating record, an anachronistic storage medium, or public proof of Italus/Farovald.
- Using a dead drop to bypass the active guardian, appoint a successor, transfer Farovald's longevity, or provide knowledge the recipient could not canonically possess in that era.
- Letting a dead drop fully reconstruct a Canon record explicitly established as destroyed or missing unless the Timeline Ledger names the bounded surviving material.
