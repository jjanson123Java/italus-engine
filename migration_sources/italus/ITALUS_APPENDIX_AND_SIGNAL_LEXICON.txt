APPENDIX A — ITALUS SIGNAL PATTERNS
============================================================

The signals of Italus are not a language.

They are biological responses interpreted by guardians through years of observation and proximity.
Communication occurs through physical signals:

• root pressure
• trunk lean
• needle movement
• resin temperature
• soil vibration
• root-network chemistry

Guardians interpret these signals through pattern recognition rather than translation.

Signals are usually interpreted as clusters rather than individual indicators.

Example:

root pressure + warm resin
= humans approaching

needle vibration + trunk lean
= weather change and directional suggestion
