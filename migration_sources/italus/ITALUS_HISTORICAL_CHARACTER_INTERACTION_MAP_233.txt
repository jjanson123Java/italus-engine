ITALUS_HISTORICAL_CHARACTER_INTERACTION_MAP
Expanded Historical Character Interaction Map
Canonical Support File for the Italus Historical Saga

PURPOSE
This document provides a stronger, historically grounded interaction layer
for the Italus saga by matching real historical figures to valid event anchors,
books, guardians, regions, and plausible scene contexts.

USAGE RULES
1. Every row must align to a valid EVENT_ID in ITALUS_HISTORICAL_EVENT_BACKBONE.
2. The listed guardian must match the guardian assigned to the event year.
3. Historical figures must be alive in the listed year or date range.
4. Historical figures may be encountered directly, indirectly, by rumor, through envoys, documents, sermons, markets, ports, camps, courts, or returning witnesses.
5. Italus and guardians may observe or interact at the edges of history, but do not alter outcomes.
