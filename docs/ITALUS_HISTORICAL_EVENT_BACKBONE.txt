ITALUS_HISTORICAL_EVENT_BACKBONE
Master Historical Timeline
Canonical Research Ledger for the Italus Saga
PURPOSE

This document is the historical backbone of the Italus saga.

It contains 180 historically verified events spanning 789 AD to 2020 AD.

These events serve as chronological anchors for the narrative and ensure
that all fictional scenes involving Italus and the guardians remain
aligned with real historical developments.

Events listed in this document must never be altered.

Italus and the guardians may observe, pass through, or be affected by
these events, but they do not influence or change historical outcomes.

This timeline must always be cross-checked with:

• the Italus Character Bible  
• the Italus World Bible  
• the Italus Saga Architecture Map

USAGE RULES
1. History remains unchanged.
2. Italus and the guardian may witness, pass through, or be affected by these events.
3. They do not alter outcomes.
4. Events should be used to shape environment, danger, travel, politics, religion, science, and culture.
5. Check guardian service dates and travel plausibility against the Character Bible, World Bible, and Saga Architecture Map.
