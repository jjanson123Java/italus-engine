ITALUS SAGA ARCHITECTURE MAP
Master Series Coherence Document
Text Edition

PURPOSE
This document provides the master series map for the Italus saga.
It is the top-level continuity guide for:
- all 11 books
- the active guardian in each book
- the major historical anchor events
- the travel route of Italus
- the emotional stage of the tree

CANON NOTE
This map must remain aligned with:
- the Italus World Bible
- the Italus Character Bible
- the Guardian Master Timeline
- the historical event backbone

SERIES COHERENCE RULES

1. The Guardian Master Timeline overrides all narrative summaries.
2. Italus never changes historical outcomes.
3. Major cities may be approached, skirted, or observed from the edges, but not entered as a rooted urban presence.
4. Every book must preserve the emotional stage assigned here.
5. Travel routes must remain compatible with Italus’s movement limits and annual regeneration requirement.
6. Historical anchor events should shape the world around Italus and the guardian, not turn them into hidden movers of history.
7. If a scene conflicts with this architecture map, this map must be checked against the World Bible and Guardian Timeline before writing proceeds.

SIGNAL ESCALATION RULE
==================================================

Each book should contain three major signal amplification
events (Memory Resonance Events).

RESONANCE PLACEMENT PATTERN

1. Early book — discovery or confusion
   The guardian first encounters Italus's intensified signal
   state. Tone: disorientation, unease, incomplete understanding.

2. Mid book — major historical disturbance
   Timed to the book's anchor historical event. Tone: weight,
   dread, sensory overload.

3. Late book — guardian bond or crisis moment
   As the guardian-Italus bond deepens or faces its greatest
   test. Tone: grief, recognition, permanence.

Resonance events must react to history, not modify it.
Resonance requires direct physical guardian contact with
Italus. Resonance is only available to guardians in Phase 3
(50+ years of service). See Guardian Resonance Availability
Table in ITALUS_KNOWLEDGE_PACK_GENERATION.

GUARDIANS WHO CANNOT CARRY RESONANCE EVENTS
Marco (970-990): 20-year service. Never reaches Phase 3.
Rosa (1900-1940): 40-year service. Never reaches Phase 3.
Theodora (1940-1985): 45-year service. Never reaches Phase 3.

ERA SIGNAL CEILING BY BOOK

Books 1-2  (789-989 AD)    Touch-contact only: RP, TL-1 to TL-3,
                            RS-1 to RS-2. Guardian feels signals
                            but does not decode them.

Books 3-5  (989-1389 AD)   Adds resin and needle movement: all RS
                            and ND signals, TL-4 to TL-5. Compound
                            clusters first interpretable.

Books 6-7  (1389-1789 AD)  Full 30-signal lexicon. Soil chemistry
                            (SC) and root network (RN) signals now
                            legible. History arrives through the
                            tree's chemistry.

Books 8-11 (1789-present)  Full lexicon plus resonance. Depth and
                            duration replace discovery. All signals
                            carried by historical weight.

WITHIN-BOOK SIGNAL DENSITY ARC

Signal scenes must escalate across chapters and cluster
around the anchor historical event. Do not distribute
signal content evenly.

  Prologue / Ch 1-2    Minimal. One or two minor signals.
                       Guardian notices but cannot explain.

  Ch 3-4               First moderate clusters. Two signal
                       types simultaneously. Historical
                       disturbance arrives in tree first.

  Ch 5                 Moderate clusters peak. Tree and human
                       tension on guardian simultaneously.

  Ch 6                 Full era vocabulary. History comes
                       through root chemistry.

  Ch 7-8               Signals settle. Aftermath chemistry.
                       Italus closing reflection belongs here.

  Ch 9-10              One moderate cluster. Resonance event
                       if this book carries one. Always
                       late-book placement.

HISTORICAL EVENT SIGNAL SEQUENCE

When a major historical event is within sensing range,
signals arrive in this biological order. Writers must not
reverse this sequence.

  1. Soil chemistry (SC signals)
     The ground records what has happened. Fires, blood,
     mass movement. Arrives hours or days before visual
     confirmation. Tier 3 and above only (Books 6+).

  2. Root network sensory (RN signals)
     Scale and type of human movement. Marching columns,
     cavalry, supply wagons. Tier 2 and above (Books 3+).

  3. Root pressure and trunk lean (RP and TL signals)
     Italus's internal alarm state. The instruction to
     the guardian: move, stop, wait. All tiers.

  4. Resin signals (RS signals)
     Sustained biological response across hours and days.
     The overnight record. All tiers.

SIGNAL SAGA EVOLUTION
==================================================

Signal understanding evolves across guardians throughout the saga.

Early guardians detect signals but struggle to interpret them.
Later guardians inherit a stronger intuitive awareness of Italus
signaling, built on the accumulated notebooks, oral transmissions,
and root-care rituals passed through the lineage.

CROSS-BOOK PROGRESSION EXAMPLE

Book 1 — Discovery
Luca has no framework. Signals are felt as animal instinct.
No named categories. No deliberate interpretation.

Book 2 — Curiosity
Elia begins systematic observation. First named signal patterns.
The guardian treats the tree as a subject of study rather than
a miracle to manage.

Book 3 — Recognition
Simonetta and Anselm inherit Elia's methods. Notebooks begin.
Resin and needle signals become reliable vocabulary.
The guardian can now read a minimal signal as intended communication.

Book 4 onward — Interpretation and Instinct
Each generation inherits richer signal vocabulary.
By the Stage 3 era (Books 6-7) soil chemistry makes the historical
world legible through the tree before the guardian's eyes confirm it.
By the Stage 4 era (Books 8-11) signal reading is so deep it operates
as instinct — the guardian knows before they reason why.

This progression creates the sense that a language between Italus and
humanity develops across centuries. The saga's signal arc is not a
technology improving — it is a relationship deepening.

CROSS-BOOK SIGNAL TONE GUIDE

Books 1-2      Signals feel like unexplained sensation. The writing
               names the physical experience without interpretation.
               Readers understand before the guardian does.

Books 3-5      Signals feel like an emerging grammar. The guardian
               begins to name what they feel. Uncertainty is still
               present but is now productive — a question the guardian
               is learning to answer.

Books 6-7      Signals feel like intelligence arriving in advance.
               The tree reports the historical world before the human
               world confirms it. Dramatic irony is at its sharpest.

Books 8-11     Signals feel like accumulated weight. The guardian
               has spoken this language for decades. Each signal
               carries the memory of all the previous times they
               felt it. Discovery is replaced by recognition.
