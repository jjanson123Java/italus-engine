PURPOSE
This document provides historically anchored scene seeds for the Italus saga.
Each row is designed to support chapter planning and AI-assisted drafting
without violating canon, chronology, guardian continuity, or geography.

USAGE RULES
1. Every scene must derive from a valid EVENT_ID in the Historical Event Backbone.
2. The listed guardian must match the backbone guardian for the event year.
3. Historical characters must be alive in the listed year and plausible for the region.
4. Italus and the guardian may observe, overhear, escort, conceal, or pass through history.
5. Italus and the guardian do not alter historical outcomes.
6. Narrative balance target:
   - 80% human interaction
   - 15% Italus sensory narration
   - 5% signal interpretation

FORMAT
