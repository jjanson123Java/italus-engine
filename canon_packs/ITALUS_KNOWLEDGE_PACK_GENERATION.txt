MASTER RULES
============================================================

1. History remains unchanged.
2. Italus and the guardians may observe, overhear, escort, conceal, survive, or pass through historical events, but they do not alter outcomes.
3. Italus does not speak.
4. Italus communicates only through physical signals:
   - root pressure
   - trunk lean
   - needle movement
   - resin temperature
   - soil vibration
5. Guardians interpret signals. Signals are never direct spoken language.
6. Italus must remain in the correct emotional stage for the year.
7. The guardian must match the correct service period for the scene year.
8. Historical figures must be alive in the scene year and geographically plausible.
9. Narrative ratio target:
   - 80% human interaction
   - 15% Italus sensory narration
   - 5% signal interpretation
10. Prose style must remain earthy, sensory, concise, and non-mystical.
11. Each generated book must include up to three Memory Resonance Events
    aligned with major narrative turning points. See Resonance Event
    Generation Rule below.

Each generated book must include up to three Memory Resonance Events

RECOMMENDED PLACEMENT

  The guardian first encounters Italus's intensified signal state.
  Tone: disorientation, wonder, unease.

• Mid-book — major historical disturbance resonance
  Timed to the book's anchor historical event (plague, earthquake,
  battle, political collapse). Tone: weight, dread, sensory overload.

• Late book — guardian bond or crisis moment resonance
  As the guardian-Italus bond deepens or faces its greatest test.
  Tone: grief, recognition, permanence.

GENERATION CONSTRAINTS

• Maximum three resonance events per book — never exceed this
• Each event requires direct physical guardian contact with Italus
• Events deliver only sensory impressions: vibration, heat, pressure,
  smell, temperature — never language, vision, or prophecy

• Resonance content must remain biologically grounded at all times
• Use the Signal Hierarchy Table (ITALUS_KNOWLEDGE_PACK_CORE,
  Appendix A-B) to calibrate signal intensity across the full book:
  minor → moderate → resonance, in ascending order of narrative weight

============================================================
SIGNAL PROGRESSION RULES
============================================================

PURPOSE
These rules govern how the signal system grows across the saga -- across
eras, within each guardian's service period, and across each book's
chapters. Writers must calibrate signal complexity and guardian literacy
against these rules before drafting any scene containing signal content.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 1 -- ERA-BASED SIGNAL LITERACY MAP
------------------------------------------------------------

The 30-signal lexicon does not become available all at once. Signal
literacy is a product of generational accumulation -- each era of
guardians perceives a deeper layer of the system than the one before.
The following table defines the ceiling of what any guardian in a given
era can reliably interpret. Signals above the era ceiling may be
physically present but will be misread, ignored, or felt as
undifferentiated unease.

BOOKS 1-2 / STAGE 1 / 789-989 AD
Signal ceiling: Touch-contact signals only.
Available signals: RP-1 through RP-6 (root pressure), TL-1 through TL-3
(trunk lean/hold), RS-1 and RS-2 (warm/cool resin flow).
Guardian literacy: Signals are not decoded -- only felt. The guardian
perceives alarm or calm. Warm bark means something approaches. A root
tightening underfoot means stop moving. Luca's hand-knock touch system
(hand on bark = stay, knock = move at nightfall) is the absolute ceiling
of deliberate two-way communication in this era. No guardian in Books 1-2
reads resin variation, needle movement, or soil chemistry as intentional
signals.

BOOKS 3-5 / STAGE 2 / 989-1410 AD
Signal ceiling: Resin signals and needle movement added.
Available signals: All Book 1-2 signals, plus RS-3 through RS-6 (all
resin signals), ND-1 through ND-5 (all needle movement signals),
TL-4 and TL-5 (exhaustion and environmental disturbance).
Guardian literacy: Guardians begin naming signals in botanical notebooks.
Elia's systematic cataloguing methods are transmitted and refined. A
guardian can now read resin temperature and needle tension to assess
terrain, weather, and energy state. First era in which compound signal
clusters (two signals simultaneously) are reliably interpreted. Single-
type soil chemistry signals appear at the end of this era (Book 5 /
Giacomo) but are not yet consistently decoded.

BOOKS 6-7 / STAGE 3 / 1410-1800 AD
Signal ceiling: Soil chemistry and root network signals become legible.
Available signals: Full lexicon of 30 signals.
Guardian literacy: Soil chemistry signals (SC-1 through SC-5) are now
read as environmental intelligence: iron means blood nearby, ash means
recent fire, nitrogen means mass burial or livestock, phosphate means
disturbed earth. The historical world begins arriving through the tree's
root chemistry. Root network sensory signals (RN-1 through RN-4)
distinguish marching columns from cavalry from wagon traffic. Domenico
(Book 7) is the first guardian to attempt to document the complete signal
vocabulary -- he never finishes, and the notebooks are destroyed in 1693.
This era is the signal system's apex of discovery. Everything is
available; nothing is taken for granted.

BOOKS 8-11 / STAGE 4 / 1800 AD-present
Signal ceiling: Full 30-signal vocabulary plus resonance events.
Guardian literacy: All signals are available, but novelty is gone.
The Stage 4 arc inverts: instead of learning new signals, the guardian
lives inside a language spoken across decades. Depth replaces discovery.
Compound clusters (moderate signals) are routine. Resonance events carry
maximum narrative weight because the guardian is fluent enough to know
what the tree is doing and still cannot fully absorb it. Stage 4 signal
writing emphasises weight, duration, and aftermath -- not surprise.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 2 -- NEW GUARDIAN ONBOARDING ARC
------------------------------------------------------------

Every new guardian -- regardless of era -- begins with no signal literacy
and must rebuild it across their service period. This applies even when
the era ceiling is high. A guardian in 1620 AD still cannot read soil
chemistry on their first day. The three-phase onboarding arc below
defines what a guardian can perceive at each point in their tenure.

This arc is the source of dramatic irony in every book's opening
chapters. The reader carries accumulated knowledge of the signal system.
The new guardian does not. That gap is where tension lives.

PHASE 1 -- FIRST YEARS (0 to approximately 10 years of service)
Signal literacy: Physical presence only.
The guardian perceives that Italus is alive and reactive but cannot
decode signals deliberately. Encounters are accidental: the bark felt
warm right before danger arrived; a root seemed to shift underfoot
before a stranger appeared. The guardian cannot yet tell alarm from
weather response. They are learning the difference between the tree's
daily biological rhythms and its intentional outputs without knowing
either category exists yet. Italus in this phase registers the new
guardian through weight, scent, and footstep pattern -- cataloguing
rather than communicating.

PHASE 2 -- MIDDLE PERIOD (approximately 10 to 50 years of service)
Signal literacy: Practical signal vocabulary within era ceiling.
Root pressure and trunk lean become legible and usable for movement and
concealment decisions. Resin signals follow. A minimal working
conversation becomes possible: the guardian can read whether it is safe
to move, whether something is approaching, whether Pollino return is
urgent. Compound clusters (two signal types simultaneously) begin to
register as meaningful. The guardian starts cross-referencing signals
with events in their notebooks. This is when the guardian bond
deepens -- not at the moment of discovery, but at the moment of first
reliable comprehension.

PHASE 3 -- LATE PERIOD (50 or more years of service)
Signal literacy: Full access to era ceiling signals; compound clusters
read fluently; resonance events available.
The guardian has accumulated enough signal experience to read the tree
the way an experienced reader reads a face -- quickly, involuntarily,
with nuance. Soil chemistry and root network signals are available if
the era permits. The guardian no longer has to focus to interpret; they
notice a resin temperature change while doing something else entirely.
This is the phase in which late-era guardians report that the bond has
become structural rather than effortful. Resonance events, when they
occur, do so in this phase -- the guardian must be experienced enough
to survive the intensity of the signal without misreading it as
supernatural.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 3 -- WITHIN-BOOK SIGNAL DENSITY ARC
------------------------------------------------------------

The 5% narrative ratio for signal interpretation is correct as a
book-level average. It must not be distributed evenly. Signal content
must escalate across a book's chapters, clustering around the historical
event and releasing in the aftermath. The following pattern maps signal
density to the standard 10-chapter pacing model.

PROLOGUE and CHAPTERS 1-2 (Establish guardian and region)
Signal density: Minimal. One or two minor signals at most.
Tone: The guardian notices something unusual but cannot explain it.
The reader feels the signal system as potential, not delivery. The tree
is present. Its meaning is not yet available to this guardian.
Example: The bark was warm on the north face at noon. There was no sun
on the north face. The guardian noted this and moved on.

CHAPTERS 3-4 (Minor historical disturbance)
Signal density: First moderate clusters begin.
Two signal types fire simultaneously. The guardian starts
cross-referencing: the resin is cooling and the roots are still. This
is not safety -- this is the stillness before something arrives. The
historical disturbance is arriving in the tree before it arrives in the
guardian's world.

CHAPTER 5 (Character tension)
Signal density: Moderate clusters at their peak frequency.
The historical pressure and the tree's biological state are both
pushing the guardian simultaneously. The guardian is managing a human
situation in the foreground while reading a compound signal cluster in
the background. This is where misreading becomes possible and
consequential.

CHAPTER 6 (Major historical event)
Signal density: Full within-era vocabulary. Soil chemistry signals
firing if the era permits. The historical world is coming through the
tree's root chemistry as armies move, cities burn, disease spreads, or
political collapse advances. The guardian reads the event through the
tree before -- or as -- it arrives in front of their eyes. The signal
sequence always runs: soil chemistry first (the ground records what has
happened), then root network sensory signals (marching, cavalry, wagons),
then resin and root pressure (Italus's internal state responding to what
it has perceived). This sequence is the biological order in which history
arrives at the tree. Writers must preserve it.

CHAPTERS 7-8 (Aftermath and travel)
Signal density: Signals settle into quieter minor and single-type.
The tree and the guardian process what has just occurred. This is the
correct placement for Italus's closing reflections -- the long view,
the chemical record of events now receding. SC-2 (nitrogen surge) and
SC-3 (ash trace) continue as the aftermath persists in the soil.

CHAPTERS 9-10 (Secondary disturbance and resolution/transition)
Signal density: Return to minor, with one moderate cluster at Ch. 9.
If a resonance event occurs in this book, it belongs at the Ch. 9-10
threshold -- the culminating guardian-tree moment before succession
or departure. Resonance events are always late-book.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 4 -- HISTORICAL EVENT SIGNAL INTEGRATION SEQUENCE
------------------------------------------------------------

When a major historical event occurs within sensing range of Italus,
the tree's biological systems register it in a fixed sequence. Writers
must follow this order.

SEQUENCE

Step 1 -- Soil chemistry signals (SC-1 through SC-5)
These arrive first. Iron concentration spikes from blood. Nitrogen
surges from mass burial or livestock movement. Ash traces from fire.
Phosphate shifts from disturbed earth. Italus knows before the guardian,
but only in chemistry -- not in narrative.

Step 2 -- Root network sensory signals (RN-1 through RN-4)
Vibration clusters from marching columns. Irregular vibration from
cavalry. Heavy rhythmic ground pressure from supply wagons. Faint
tremor waves from seismic precursors. These signals tell Italus the
scale and type of human movement approaching or receding.

Step 3 -- Root pressure and trunk lean (RP and TL signals)
Italus's own internal response to what it has perceived. Root
tightening is alarm. Trunk lean is directional preference. Root
stillness is the signal to hide. These are the signals that translate
into guardian action.

Step 4 -- Resin signals (RS-1 through RS-6)
Italus's sustained biological response across hours and days.
Warm resin confirms human proximity. Heavy resin bleed signals
injury or root stress. Cool secretion signals the return of safety.
The resin record is what guardians find in their notebooks the next
morning -- the overnight account of what the tree experienced.

WRITER GUIDANCE
A guardian who wakes to find heavy resin bleed and ash trace in the
soil chemistry has just been told, in the tree's biological language,
that violence occurred nearby overnight. They did not need to witness
it. This is the mechanism by which the tree and the guardian share
history without either of them being able to change it.

SIGNAL ESCALATION RULE
============================================================

Signal intensity must increase gradually across the narrative.

Early chapters should primarily use minor signals. Mid-book chapters
may include moderate signals tied to historical disturbances.
Resonance events should only appear after the guardian relationship
with Italus has been established across sufficient service years.

Each generated book may include a maximum of three Memory Resonance
Events.

Recommended placement:

• Early book — discovery or confusion resonance
  The guardian first encounters Italus's intensified signal state.
  They do not yet have the vocabulary to name what happened.

• Mid-book — major historical disturbance resonance
  Timed to the book's anchor historical event. The signal system
  delivers history through the tree before the guardian's eyes
  confirm it.

• Late book — guardian bond or crisis moment resonance
  As the guardian-Italus bond reaches its deepest point or faces
  its greatest test. The resonance event at this position carries
  the full weight of the guardian's accumulated signal literacy.

See Signal Progression Rules (this document) for era ceilings,
guardian onboarding phases, Guardian Resonance Availability Table,
and chapter signal density arc.
